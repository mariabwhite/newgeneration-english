Third person · "She works" ⭐

the -s rule · 6 models

The one delicate point in the Present Simple: with he / she / it we add -s to the verb. Everything else in this tense is quite straightforward.

Rule: I / you / we / they — verb stays the same.   ·   he / she / it — + s at the end.
I work → She works · I drink → He drinks · I go → She goes

Spelling -s · 4 rules

4 rules + 6 tap-quiz

Why goes and not gos? Why studies and not studys? Four rules follow, and then a brief quiz for practice.

§ 1 · default
+ s
Most verbs — we simply add -s.
work → works · live → lives · like → likes · start → starts
§ 2 · after -o, -s, -x, -ch, -sh
+ es
Otherwise the sound is hard to say — we add -es.
go → goes · do → does · watch → watches · finish → finishes · miss → misses
§ 3 · consonant + y
y → ies
If a consonant comes before y — change it to ies.
study → studies · try → tries · fly → flies · cry → cries
§ 4 · vowel + y
just + s
If a VOWEL comes before y — keep the y, just add -s.
play → plays · say → says · buy → buys · enjoy → enjoys
§ 5 · exception
have → has
The only real exception. Memorise it.
She has a small office. He has two colleagues.
⚠ How -s sounds:
• [s] voiceless — after voiceless consonants: works · stops · likes · waits
• [z] voiced — after voiced consonants and vowels: lives · plays · goes · comes
• [ɪz] "iz" — after -s / -ch / -sh / -x / -ge: watches · finishes · misses · fixes
He ? to the gym on Monday.
go → goes (rule §2: -o + es).
She ? English every day.
study → studies (consonant + y → ies).
My colleague ? a big office.
have → has (exception §5).
She ? football with her son.
watch → watches (rule §2: -ch + es).
He ? tennis every Saturday.
play — vowel + y → just plays. NOT plaies.
The office ? at 9 a.m.
office = it → opens.

Negative · don't / doesn't

rule + 6 models + 4 mcq

How do we express "I do NOT do"? Not with "no" — kindly use don't. With he / she / it — doesn't.

Formula: I / you / we / they — don't + verb.   ·   he / she / it — doesn't + verb WITHOUT -s.
I don't drink coffee.   ·   She doesn't drink coffee. (NOT doesn't drinks)
I don't work on Sunday.

Time in Present Simple · at / on / in

rule + 6 models

The Present Simple travels together with time prepositions. A brief recap: at — an exact time; on — days; in — longer periods.

AT · exact: at 7 a.m. · at noon · at night
ON · days: on Monday · on Friday morning · on the weekend
IN · periods: in the morning · in May · in winter · in 2026
I start work at 9 a.m.at + exact time
I go to the gym on Monday.on + day
I don't work on Sunday.negative + on + day
In the evenings I read at home.in + period
She goes to the office at 8:30.3rd person + at
He works from home on Friday.3rd person + on
