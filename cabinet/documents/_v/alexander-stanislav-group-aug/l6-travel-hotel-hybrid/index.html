☕ Card 11 · Would you like some…? · 10 situations

Formulas

Offer: Would you like some coffee? · Would you like a coffee? · Can I get you some water?
Accept: Yes, please. · That would be great. · I'd love some.
Refuse: No, thank you. · I'm fine, thanks. · Not right now, thanks anyway.

10 situations · Alexander is HOST, Stanislav is GUEST · then swap

За каждую сцену: HOST предлагает 2-3 разные вещи (используй some). GUEST — принимает одно, вежливо отказывается от второго, задаёт 1 встречный вопрос.

1. A foreign partner is your guest at the office. First 5 min in the lobby. -> offer some coffee / some tea / some water.
2. At a café with your partner. You sit first, order first. -> offer some coffee / a croissant / some tea.
3. Morning at the office. Partner has just arrived. -> offer some coffee / some sugar / some milk.
4. After lunch in the meeting room. -> offer some water / more coffee / a mint.
5. Partner is tired · 2-hour meeting. -> offer a break / some coffee / a snack.
6. In the car on the way to the airport. -> offer some music / some water / a mint.
7. On the plane · partner next to you. -> offer half of your croissant / your bottle of water / your magazine.
8. In the waiting lounge. -> offer coffee to go / a sandwich / a newspaper.
9. Dinner at a restaurant. -> offer some wine / some bread / a starter.
10. Late in the office · past 20:00. -> offer some coffee / some tea / a short break.

Words · use each ≥ 3 times

Would you like some …? Would you like a …? Can I get you …? Yes, please That would be great I'd love some No, thank you I'm fine, thanks Thanks anyway some coffee / tea / water / sugar / milk / bread / wine
⚠ some — в вежливых предложениях («Would you like some tea?»), не «any».

«It is Monday morning. Alex arrives at the office at 7:45. Good morning! he says to the receptionist. His first meeting is at nine. At lunchtime he has a quick coffee with Stan. Target words to hear in the questions / answers

Story 2 · A tricky partner

«Our new business partner in Berlin is a strange man. On paper, he protects the deal and values a long-term relationship. In practice, he never replies to my emails on time, but always answers his phone in three seconds. Every quarter he borrows a large sum from our supplier and does not return it on time. Some people say he tries to deceive previous partners. In the worst rumour, he steals or even launders money. I have no evidence — only a feeling.»

Task 2 · make 5 questions on Story 2 (Stanislav asks, Alexander answers · then swap)

Present Simple only. Use: Why / What / Who / How often / Where.

Example: «Why does he answer the phone so fast?»

Target verbs · 110-116

to protect to value to borrow to reply to answer to deceive to steal to launder money

Story 3 · My travel routine · airport day

«I always travel light. My luggage is a small trolley-bag. I check in three hours in advance, take my boarding pass, and go to the gate. In the waiting lounge I reply to emails for one hour. When my flight is announced, I find a seat — usually by the window. It is not expensive, and for a busy week it is very beneficial. If it is my first time abroad in that country, I find a way to the hotel easily. Sometimes the schedule is confusing, but customs is quick, and my flight is rarely delayed.»

Task 3 · make 6 questions on Story 3 · use these patterns

How long does it take you to check in?
How long is the way to the nearest gate?
Why do you check in three hours in advance?
What takes you so long at customs?

Target airport words

abroad waiting lounge flight check-in a boarding pass a gate a seat easily to find a way beneficial expensive cheap customs confusing schedule in advance
⚠ For the question patterns: How long does it take + you + to do? / Why do you …? / What takes you so long?
