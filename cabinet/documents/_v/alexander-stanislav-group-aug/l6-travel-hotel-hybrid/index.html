🎬 Live now · what is happening at the airport

Look around at the airport right now. What do you see? For actions happening in this moment, English uses a special form: am / is / are + verb-ing. Оглянитесь вокруг в аэропорту прямо сейчас. Что Вы видите? Для действий в этот момент в английском есть форма: am / is / are + глагол-ing. Не путайте с обычным Present Simple ("I fly to Berlin twice a year" = вообще, а "I am flying to Berlin right now" = сейчас, в этот момент).

Mini-rule (2 seconds):
• I am + verb-ing  →  I am boarding.
• He / She / It is + verb-ing  →  The plane is landing.
• We / You / They are + verb-ing  →  We are waiting at the gate.
Три строчки — вся суть Present Continuous.

📐 Story Card · your travel story · 3 beats

Any good travel story fits into three beats: Setup · Trouble · Resolution. Same shape as your K02 monolog, just about a trip. This is a scaffold for the homework — say it aloud once now, polish it at home. Любая travel-история укладывается в три бита: Setup · Trouble · Resolution. Та же форма, что и Ваш монолог из K02, только про поездку. Это каркас для домашки — проговорите один раз сейчас, отшлифуете дома.

📐 3-beat frame
Beat 1 · Setup: where and when. "Last spring I was flying from Moscow to Berlin for a business meeting…"
Beat 2 · Trouble: what went wrong. "…but at the gate they announced a two-hour delay, and my suitcase was on a different flight."
Beat 3 · Resolution: how it ended. "In the end I put on the same shirt for two days, borrowed a charger, and still made the meeting on time."
Phrase bank for your story (mix K02 + today):
• Setup: "Last spring I was flying from … to … for a business trip."
• Trouble: "Suddenly the gate closed early / my luggage did not arrive / the taxi was two hours late."
• Resolution: "In the end I found a way, arrived at the hotel, and reached the meeting easily."
Connectors: but · suddenly · in the end · finally · so · thanks to that.
Try it now · 60 seconds each: Alexander goes first — one real (or invented) travel story in 3 beats. Then Stanislav. No script — full sentences only. Проговорите сейчас: Александр — своя (или придуманная) history в 3 бита, 60 секунд. Потом Станислав.

Two sides, 60 seconds each. Pick the side you honestly prefer, argue it aloud. Then switch and argue the other side (harder — but better English). Vocab from K02 that fits: seat · window · aisle · flight · in advance · beneficial · expensive · cheap. Две стороны, 60 секунд каждая. 🪟 Side A · Window seat
You can look outside and relax.
Nobody wakes you up — you can sleep the whole flight.
The wall is a good pillow.
Better for photos of the city on landing.
🚶 Side B · Aisle seat
