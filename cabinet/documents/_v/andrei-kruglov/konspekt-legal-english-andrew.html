Standing · three-part test: (1) concrete injury · (2) traceable to defendant's conduct · (3) redressable by the court. If any leg is missing, the plaintiff lacks standing no matter how strongly it feels.

F · Target constructions · formal patterns 6 constructions

Источник: §14 Target Constructions · §15 Transformation drill × 15.

1. Conditional inversion

Were + S + to + inf, … would …  ·  Should + S + inf, …
Formal replacement for If-clauses. Were the court to reverse its ruling, the entire industry would redraft its contracts. · Should the defendant fail to appear, the court will proceed in absentia.

2. Subjunctive after suggest / essential / vital / crucial

It is essential that + S + (should) + base verb  ·  suggest + that + S + (should) + base verb
Third-person singular drops the -s. It is essential that Marta's counsel document every lost sale. · The mediator suggested that Golden Crust pay a running royalty.

3. Inversion · formal emphasis

Rarely / Seldom + aux + S + …  ·  Never + aux + S + …  ·  Not only + aux + S + …, but also …  ·  No sooner + aux + S + … than …
Aux must agree with tense (did / does / had / have). Not only did Golden Crust copy the pattern, but it also reproduced the label font. · Never had the firm faced a class action of this scale. · No sooner had the settlement been announced than the share price rebounded.

4. Cleft sentences · focus and formality

What + S + V + is/was + [focus]  ·  It is/was + [focus] + who / that …
Lands emphasis on the focus phrase. What my client is really asking for is a withdrawal of the lookalike loaf. · It is the arbitration clause that quietly removes the right to a jury trial.

5. Academic linkers

therefore, · consequently, · on the other hand, · whereas
The claim lacked documentary evidence; therefore, the judge dismissed it. · Mediation seeks agreement, whereas arbitration ends with a binding decision.

6. «Suggest» constructions · four shapes

suggest + noun / gerund  ·  suggest + that + S + (should) + base verb  ·  suggest + wh-word clause  ·  suggest + something + to + someone
Never: I suggest you to see a doctor ❌ — suggest doesn't take someone + to-infinitive. Correct: I suggest that you see a doctor.
