1.Verb to be · am / is / are ↑

ЛицоПолнаяКороткаяПример
IamI'mI am at home. / I'm tired.
you / we / theyareyou're / we're / they'reThey are here. / We're ready.
he / she / itishe's / she's / it'sHe is a manager. / It's cold.

Отрицание · вопрос · короткий ответ

  • Отрицание: I am not / isn't / aren't. «She isn't here.»
  • Вопрос: Am I…? Is he…? Are you…? — начинаем с be.
  • Short answer: «Are you tired? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.»

2.Present Simple · обычные действия ↑

Для повторяющихся действий, привычек, фактов. Триггеры: every day, usually, often, always, never, on Sundays.

Формы

УтверждениеОтрицаниеВопрос
I / You / We / They workdon't workDo I / you / we / they work?
He / She / It works (-s)doesn't workDoes he / she / it work? (без -s)
Различение. «Does she works?» ❌ — после does глагол теряет -s: Does she work? ✓

3.Present Continuous · прямо сейчас ↑

Действие в момент речи. Триггеры: now, at the moment, right now, look!, listen!

Формула: am / is / are + V-ing. I am wearing a jacket. She is drinking coffee. They are working.

4.Present Simple vs Continuous ↑

SimpleContinuous
каждый день, обычно, фактпрямо сейчас, в моменте
I drink coffee every morning.I am drinking coffee right now.
She works in an office.She is working from home today.

Stative verbs · НЕ ставим в Continuous

know · understand · want · need · like · love · hate · prefer · believe · remember · have (в значении «иметь»).

Уточнение. «I am liking coffee» ❌ → «I like coffee» ✓. «He is having a car» ❌ → «He has a car» ✓.

5.Вопросы · Do/Does · to be · Wh- ↑

Общий вопрос (Yes / No)

  • С обычным глаголом: Do you drink coffee? · Does she work here?
  • С to be: Are you tired? · Is he a manager?
  • С модальным: Can you swim? · Can I try this on?

Wh-вопросы · формула

Wh-word + do/does + subject + verb? — «Where do you live?» С to be — без do: «Where is the shop?»

Wh-словоЗонаПример
WhatвещьWhat do you drink in the morning?
WhereместоWhere do you work?
When / What timeвремяWhat time do you get up?
WhoчеловекWho is your favourite writer?
WhyпричинаWhy do you take a taxi?
HowспособHow do you pay?
How much / How manyцена / количествоHow much is this? How many chairs are there?
How oftenчастотаHow often do you go shopping?

6.There is / There are · It is ↑

  • There is + один предмет: There is a lamp on the table.
  • There are + много: There are four chairs in the kitchen.
  • It is + погода / время / оценка: It is cold today. · It is 5 o'clock. · It is delicious.
Уточнение. «On the table is a lamp» ❌ → «There is a lamp on the table» ✓ (начинаем с there).

7.Have got · Can / Can't ↑

  • Have got — что у тебя есть. I have got a car. Have you got a car? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
  • Has got — для he/she/it. She has got two children.
  • Can / Can't — умею / не умею. I can drive but I can't swim.

8.Frequency adverbs · always → never ↑

%СловоПример
100alwaysI always drink coffee in the morning.
~90usuallyShe usually works from home.
~70oftenWe often eat out on Fridays.
~50sometimesI sometimes take a taxi.
~10seldom / rarelyHe seldom drinks alcohol.
0neverI never smoke.
Место. Перед обычным глаголом — «I always drink coffee.» После be — «She is never late.»

9.Множественное число · Apostrophe 's ↑

Regular · +s / +es

a book → books · a box → boxes · a city → cities · a wife → wives.

Irregular · запомнить

manmenwomanwomen
childchildrenpersonpeople
footfeettoothteeth
mousemicegoosegeese
fishfishsheepsheep

Apostrophe 's

  • Единственное — Denis's car · my sister's book.
  • Множественное с -s — my parents' house · the students' books.
  • Нерегулярное множественное — children's toys · people's opinions.

РядомДалеко
Одинthis jacketthat jacket

«Can I try these sneakers on?» (в руках) · «How much is that jacket over there?» (на манекене)

11.Future · will vs going to ↑

  • will — решение прямо сейчас. «— This bag is heavy. — I'll help you.»
  • going to — план, уже есть намерение. «Tomorrow I am going to buy a new coat — I know which shop.»
  • Present Continuous · future — назначенное событие. «I'm meeting John at 5.»

12.Артикли · a / an / the — по звуку, не по букве ↑

a — перед согласным звуком

a computer · a book · a European client (звук /j/) · a university (/j/) · a one-time offer (/w/) · a hospital.

an — перед гласным звуком

an apple · an interview · an hour (h немая → гласный /ˈaʊə/) · an honest man · an MP3 («эм-пи-три»).

the — когда собеседник знает, о чём речь

  • Единственный — the sun · the moon · the president · the UK · the USA.
  • Уже упоминалось — «I bought a jacket. The jacket is warm.»
  • Понятно из контекста — «Close the door.» (эта дверь).
  • С superlative — the best · the worst · the most expensive.
Zero article (без артикля). Имена, страны (не UK/USA), общие продукты (I like coffee), спорт (I play hockey), дни / месяцы (on Monday · in July), «go to work / school / bed» без артикля.

13.Предлоги места · in / on / at ↑

ПредлогСмыслПримеры
inвнутри объёмаin Moscow · in Russia · in the kitchen · in the car · in a box
onна поверхности / линииon the table · on the wall · on Tverskaya street · on the second floor
atконкретная точкаat 47 Bishopsgate · at work · at the meeting · at home · at the airport

Пары-ловушки

  • at home — «дома» · in the house — физически внутри дома.
  • at work — «на работе» · in the office — в офисе (в помещении).
  • on the bus / train — общественный · in the car / taxi — личный.
  • in the corner — угол комнаты · on the corner — угол улицы.

14.Wh-questions · deep dive · 10 типов ↑

Разбор урока 9.08 (Ask Me Anything). Формулы + ловушки.

ТипФормулаПример
обычный глагол · PresentWh + do/does + S + verb?Where do you live?
обычный глагол · PastWh + did + S + verb?What did you buy?
to beWh + am/is/are + S?Where is the shop?
модальныйWh + can/should + S + verb?When can you meet?
have gotWh + have/has + S + got?How many jackets have you got?
Формула. «Where you live?» — забыли do. Правильно: Where do you live? С to be, наоборот, do не нужно.

S1.Shopping-clichés · 6 категорий · готовые фразы ↑

👋 Assistant asksCan I help you? · Are you looking for anything in particular? · Take your time. · Let me know if you need any help.
🙋 Your replyI'm just browsing, thanks. · I'm looking for a warm jacket. · Have you got this in size L? · Do you have this in dark blue?
👕 Trying onCan I try this on, please? · Where is the fitting room? · It's too tight / loose. · Have you got a bigger / smaller size? · This one fits perfectly.
💰 Price & discountHow much is it? · Is it on sale? · Is there a discount today? · Do you have a loyalty card? · Is tax included?
💳 At the cash deskI'll take this one, please. · Card or cash? · Do you take card? · Can I have a bag? · Would you like a receipt? · Yes, by email if possible.
🔁 Return · exchangeI'd like to return this. · Can I exchange it for a bigger size? · Here's the receipt. · Sorry, the zip is broken. · Refund or store credit?
Логика. В магазине почти никогда не строится новое предложение — используется готовая фраза из категорий выше. Ответ начинается с одной из этих фраз, а не с односложных «yes / no».

S2.Café / restaurant · заказ + оплата ↑

Barista:What can I get you?
You:A large cappuccino, please. To go.
Barista:Any milk or sugar?
You:Regular milk, no sugar, thanks.
Barista:Anything to eat with that? Croissant, muffin?
You:No, thanks. Just the coffee today.
Barista:That'll be four pounds fifty. Card or cash?
You:Card, please. Contactless.

S3.Ориентация в городе · спросить и объяснить дорогу ↑

  • Спросить: Excuse me, how do I get to the nearest metro? · Which way is the market? · Is it far from here?
  • Ответить: Go straight for two blocks · Turn left at the corner · It's on your right · It's about five minutes on foot.
  • Поблагодарить: Thanks a lot! · Cheers, appreciate it!

S4.Cash-desk dialogue · Denis + Cashier · 14 линий ↑

Cashier:Good afternoon! That's everything?
Denis:Yes, that's it, thanks.
Cashier:The jacket is on sale — twenty percent off, so eighty pounds instead of a hundred.
Denis:Oh, great — I didn't know. Even better.
Cashier:Have you got a loyalty card?
Denis:No, I don't. How does it work?
Cashier:You collect points. Every ten pounds — one point back. Free to sign up.
Denis:Sure, sign me up, please.
Cashier:Perfect. Card or cash?
Denis:Card, please. Contactless.
Cashier:Would you like a bag? Five pence.
Denis:Yes, please. And can I have the receipt by email?
Cashier:Of course. Enjoy your Saturday!
Denis:Thanks, you too.

§Направления на сентябрь ↑

  • Past Simple в живой речи — форма знакома; следующий шаг — использование в бытовых историях.
  • Спонтанная разговорная речь — переход от подготовленного ответа к неподготовленному. Формы: role-play, timer monologue.
  • Travel / hotel / airport — соседний блок сервисной лексики, продолжает shopping.
  • Развёрнутые ответы — 2 предложения с деталью вместо односложных «yes / no».
  • Аудирование — короткие сцены (café, shop, taxi, hotel), затем диалоги на 2-3 минуты.
