2.7 Softer patterns · вежливые замены

Вместо Coffee! → Could I have a coffee, please?

2.8 Present Simple questions · Do / Does

Два открывателя вопроса · ничего больше

I / you / we / they: Do + подлежащее + verb?

he / she / it: Does + подлежащее + verb (без -s на глаголе — does забирает -s себе).

Примеры:

  • Do you like coffee? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
  • Does Marco live in Rome? — No, he doesn't. He lives in Milan.
  • Where do you work? — I work in marketing.
  • What does she do? — She's a shop assistant.

Short answers: Yes, I do / No, I don't · Yes, he does / No, he doesn't.

Ловушка

  • ❌ Does he lives in Milan? → ✅ Does he live in Milan? (-s уходит к does)

3.7 Questions · общие и специальные + WH-drill

Две формы английского вопроса

General (yes/no) — ответ "да/нет". Начинаем с helper: Do / Does / Did / Is / Are / Was / Were / Can / Will.

  • Do you have Wi-Fi here? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
  • Is Marco busy right now? → Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.

Special (WH-) — запрашивает информацию. Начинаем с WH-слова, потом helper.

4.5 Present Continuous · the new piece

Present Continuous · главное новое

Форма: am / is / are + V-ing

Person+–?
II'm readingI'm not readingAm I reading?
he / she / itshe's workingshe isn't workingIs she working?
we / you / theywe're waitingwe aren't waitingAre we waiting?

Signal words: right now · at the moment · currently · this week · today · still

-ing орфография:

  • work → working (просто +ing)
  • take → taking (убираем немую -e)
  • stop → stopping (удваиваем согласную после короткого гласного: stop, run, swim, sit)
  • lie → lying (-ie → -ying)

Stative verbs — в PC не ставим

  • know · like · love · hate · want · need · understand · believe · remember · own · have (= own)
  • ✅ I know Paris. ❌ I'm knowing Paris.

В. Present Continuous — L4

Форма: am / is / are + V-ing

Signal: right now · at the moment · currently · this week · today · still

Stative verbs не берут PC: know, like, love, hate, want, need, understand, believe, remember, own, have (= own).

Г. be going to — L1 mini-trainer + L3

Для: ближайшие планы, которые уже решены.

Форма: subject + am/is/are + going to + verb

  • I'm going to get a SIM card.
  • I'm going to call him at ten.
  • She's going to visit her parents on Monday.

В речи часто слышится «gonna», но пишем и диктуем «going to».

Д. Questions — L2, L3

General: Do/Does/Did/Is/Are/Was/Were/Can/Will + subject + verb?

Special: WH + helper + subject + verb?

WH-слова: What · Where · When · Why · Who · How (+ What time, How long, How much)

⚠Типичные ошибки · «так не говорят»

Present Simple · -s и helper

  • ❌ Does he lives in Milan? → ✅ Does he live in Milan? (-s уходит к does)
  • ❌ She don't like coffee. → ✅ She doesn't like coffee.

Questions · порядок слов

  • ❌ Where you are? → ✅ Where are you?
  • ❌ What time is? → ✅ What time is it? (подлежащее it)
  • ❌ You have Wi-Fi? → ✅ Do you have Wi-Fi?
  • ❌ Who do you call? если «кто» = subject → ✅ Who calls you?
  • ❌ What time you are free? → ✅ What time are you free?

free / busy — это прилагательные

  • ❌ I am freely from three to six. → ✅ I'm free from three to six.
  • ❌ I am no busy right now. → ✅ I'm not busy right now.
  • ❌ I have meeting at eight. → ✅ I have a meeting at eight.

be going to · форма

  • ❌ I going to get a SIM card. → ✅ I'm going to get a SIM card.
  • ❌ I just arrive here. → ✅ I just arrived here.
  • ❌ Is no Wi-Fi here. → ✅ There is no Wi-Fi here.
  • ❌ I'm in Paris these week. → ✅ I'm in Paris this week.

Present Continuous · stative verbs

  • ❌ I'm knowing Paris. → ✅ I know Paris.
  • ❌ I'm wanting coffee. → ✅ I want coffee.
  • ❌ She's liking it. → ✅ She likes it.

Национальности · ловушки

  • ❌ I'm Germanian. → ✅ I'm German.
  • ❌ I'm Spainish. → ✅ I'm Spanish.
  • ❌ I'm from the UK. I'm English. (если Шотландия/Уэльс) → ✅ I'm British.

a / an перед профессией

  • ❌ I'm engineer. → ✅ I'm an engineer. (гласный звук → an)
  • ❌ I'm accountant. → ✅ I'm an accountant.
  • ✅ I'm a teacher / a manager / a lawyer. (согласный звук → a)
