3Что прошли содержательно

L3 The Call · Paris — вторая половина урока

  • Timing & scheduling: time distance (in two hours), exact clock (at six Moscow time), edges of the day (early in the morning · after nine pm · till late at night)
  • Busy / free: open window (I'm free from three to six) vs closed (I have a meeting at eight · I'm busy right now); вопросы What time are you free? / When can we talk?
  • Phone-call phrases: Can I call you back?, Give me a call, I'll call you when I get home, Only on Wi-Fi, I'll be in a Wi-Fi zone
  • Short-trip life: I'm in Paris this week, I just arrived here, I don't have mobile data yet, I'm going to get a SIM card, There is no Wi-Fi here
  • Business trip & referrals: I'm on a business trip, I'll be back on Monday, I just got back, My friend recommended you, I was told to reach out to you
  • Polite softeners: If possible, That would be great, No problem, Sorry about that, I appreciate it
  • Грамматика: be going to для ближайших планов + формула am/is/are + going to + verb

L3 Questions · общие и специальные (расширение)

  • Formula: WH-word + helper + subject + main verb? — What do you want to drink?
  • Шесть WH-слов: What, Where, When, Why, Who, How (+ производные: What time, How long, How much)
  • Yes/no openers: Do/Does/Did/Is/Are/Was/Were/Can/Will на первое место
  • 4 типовые ловушки: ❌ Where you are? → ✅ Where are you?  ·  ❌ What time is? → ✅ What time is it?  ·  ❌ You have Wi-Fi? → ✅ Do you have Wi-Fi?  ·  ❌ Who do you call? → ✅ Who calls you? (когда Who = subject)
  • Workbook · 10 drills: pick-the-WH gap-fill (10), build-the-question (4 general + 4 special), задай вопрос к выделенному слову (10), перевод с русского (10)

L4 Hôtel Léonie · Paris — новый урок

  • Scene A · Reception: check-in диалог + 12 слов (reservation, passport, breakfast included, dining room, ground floor, key card, lift, Wi-Fi, password, stay); базовые фразы Do you have a reservation?, May I see your passport, please?, Are you paying by card or in cash?, Is breakfast included?, Enjoy your stay!
  • Scene B · Café с Marco: 10 фраз для right-now и this-week small-talk (How is your week going?, Sorry I'm late — I'm finishing a call, I'm just reading the menu, A bit of both, What are you planning for tomorrow?, I'm thinking about the Louvre)
  • Grammar · Present Simple refresh: рутины / постоянные факты / расписания; signal words every day · usually · often · on Mondays · twice a week
  • Grammar · Present Continuous (новое): форма am/is/are + V-ing; signal words right now · at the moment · currently · this week · today · still; ловушки в -ing (take → taking, stop → stopping, lie → lying)
  • Stative verbs — не берут PC: know, like, love, hate, want, need, understand, believe, remember, own, have (= own). I know Paris, а не I'm knowing Paris.
  • PS vs PC · decision tree: «around now / changing?» → PC. «always / in general?» → PS.
  • Отработка: Drill PS-1 (6 items) + Drill PC-1 (6 items) + Drill PC-2 make-the-question (5 items) + MCQ PS/PC (8 items) + Builder (5 items) + Reading «Paris, Day 1» с T/F/NS (6 statements)
