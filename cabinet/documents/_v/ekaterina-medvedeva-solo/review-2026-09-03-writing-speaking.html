Task 1

Письмо Бену · «Time out» (о фильмах)

personal letter · 100-120 слов

Твой текст

Dear Ben, Thanks for your email. I'm glad to hear from you again. Sorry I havent written you for so long. I was very busy with my school. In your email you asked me about films. I'm going to answer your questions. Personaly I really like comedies and romantic films. These genres are my favorite, I usualy enjoy that kinds of films. I prefer to go to the cinema, because of its atmosphere vibe. The smell of popcorn, the ligh is off make a special atmosphere. Honestly, it If I had a chance to make a film, it would be a romantic comedy. with happy ending I will have a happy ending, because what comedy have a sad ending, right? Anyway, I have to help my mother with cooking. Write back soon. Best wishes, Kate

Формат

  • Приветствие: Dear Ben, — есть.
  • Благодарность: Thanks for your email. I'm glad to hear from you again. — есть.
  • Извинение: Sorry I havent written you for so long. — есть. Апостроф отсутствует.
  • Ответ на 3 вопроса: films (comedies + romantic), cinema, romantic comedy — раскрыто.
  • Мост: Anyway, I have to help my mother with cooking. — есть.
  • Завершающая: Write back soon. Best wishes, — есть.
  • Подпись: Kate — есть, регистр корректен.
  • Second Conditional: «If I had a chance to make a film, it would be a romantic comedy» — построен корректно.
  • Само-исправление: «with happy ending» зачёркнуто, вставлено «a happy ending».

Monologue #1 · Sculpture

2 min · a hobby I would like to try

Расшифровка (85 сек)

Today I would like to give a talk about hobbies I would like to try. My dream hobby is sculpture. I think it's very interesting and specific hobby. I find this hobby interesting because I like to — I like making something with something beautiful with my hands. Sculptures are really beautiful things that humans create, so I'd like to try it one day. For start sculpturing, people need sticks and, for example, clay, special clay, and it's all they need — and their hands, of course. Um, I plan to try it very… [пауза между записями] liked it, so I will try it as soon as I can. That's all, that's all I wanted to say. Thanks for your attention.

Что есть в тексте

  • Вступление: «Today I would like to give a talk about hobbies I would like to try» — полное.
  • Тема: sculpture — названа.
  • Причина: «I like making something beautiful with my hands» — сформулирована.
  • Что нужно: «sticks, clay, special clay, and their hands» — перечислено.
  • План попробовать: «I plan to try it… I will try it as soon as I can» — есть.
  • Заключение: «That's all I wanted to say. Thanks for your attention.» — полное.
  • Само-правка: «I like to — I like making» — присутствует.

Monologue #2 · Free-time activities of Russian teenagers

2 min · trending topic

Расшифровка (114 сек)

Today I would give a talk about free time activities of Russian teenagers. I think that the most popular activities among Russian teenagers are computer gaming, football and maybe skateboarding. Actually, teenagers don't have a lot of free time after school during the school year because most of them spend a lot of time for studying. But even those teens who have hobbies and really love them spend even one hour a day for their hobbies and activities. I don't think that computers and smartphones spoil their real hobbies because some teens may have hobbies connected with phones or computers. For example, gaming or making edits. So I don't think that's a problem. Honestly, my favorite way to spend my free time is just chilling, for example, watching movies and series, but I also like to ride a bike for real activities. That's all I wanted to say. Thanks for your attention.

Что есть в тексте

  • Вступление: «Today I would give a talk about free time activities of Russian teenagers» — есть, «like to» отсутствует.
  • Аспект 1 (популярные виды): computer gaming, football, skateboarding — перечислены.
  • Аспект 2 (мало времени): «teenagers don't have a lot of free time after school» — раскрыто.
  • Аспект 3 (влияние компьютеров): «I don't think that computers and smartphones spoil their real hobbies» — раскрыто.
  • Аспект 4 (свой выбор): «my favorite way to spend my free time is just chilling, watching movies and series, but I also like to ride a bike» — есть.
  • Заключение: «That's all I wanted to say. Thanks for your attention.» — полное.

Что править

Что былоКак надоПочему
I would give a talkI would like to give a talkПотерян «like to» в первой строке — сама frame-фраза чуть кривая. Полная форма обязательна.
spend a lot of time for studyingspend a lot of time on studyingС spend time идёт предлог on (не for).
spend even one hour a day for their hobbiesspend at least one hour a day on their hobbies«Even one hour» дословно = «даже один час» — в англ. семантика другая. «At least» лучше передаёт мысль.
for real activitiesfor something more active / for outdoor activities«Real activities» звучит как калька — противопоставляешь чиллу, но не хватает точности.
my favoritemy favouriteBritish English — стандарт ОГЭ (уже говорили в письме).
just chillingjust relaxing at home«Chilling» на экзамене на грани сленга. Экзаменатор может снять балл за style.
no structural connectorsFirst, / Second, / Also, / To sum up,Есть Actually / But / So / Honestly — это норм, но добавь 1-2 явных First / To sum up для K2.

Monologue #3 · Free time · вторая попытка

1:48 · та же тема, другой заход

Расшифровка (1:48)

Today I would like to give a talk about free time activities of Russian teenagers. I think the most popular activities among Russian teenagers today is dancing. It's very interesting for girls and boys. They have classes with their friends, they give fun, and dancing is a really cool sport. I think it's necessary to usually spend a lot of time after school. Sometimes they don't have any time to do their life, they have free time. Well, for us and the computers, it is important, it is very important, and complements a teenager's life, and maybe computer games will be activities for any teenagers. My favorite way to spend free time is mobile, because I think it is my favorite sport, and I feel connection with these activities.

Что есть в тексте

  • Вступление: «Today I would like to give a talk about free time activities of Russian teenagers» — полное.
  • Аспект 1 (популярные виды): dancing — назван.
  • Аспект 2 (нехватка времени): «Sometimes they don't have any time to do their life» — попытка, сформулировано некорректно.
  • Аспект 3 (компьютеры): «Well, for us and the computers, it is important» — попытка, сформулировано некорректно.
  • Аспект 4 (свой выбор): «My favorite way to spend free time is mobile» — есть, слово некорректно.
  • Заключения OGE-канона нет.
  • Лексика: «complements a teenager's life» — присутствует.

Что править

Что былоКак надоПочему
the most popular activities is dancingthe most popular activity is dancing (или activities are)Подлежащее и глагол должны быть в одном числе. Проще: единственный самый популярный вид → the most popular activity is dancing.
they give funit is fun / it's a lot of fun«Give fun» — калька. По-английски развлечение is fun, не даётся.
it's necessary to usually spend a lot of time after schoolthey usually have to spend a lot of time studying after schoolЛогика сломалась: «необходимо обычно тратить время после школы» = что необходимо? Учиться. Скажи это прямо.
Sometimes they don't have any time to do their life, they have free timeSometimes they don't have any free time at all«Do their life» — калька («заниматься своей жизнью»). И противоречие: «нет времени, но есть свободное время» — сама себя запутала.
Well, for us and the computers, it is importantAs for us and computers, they are very important…«For us and the computers» = грамматически «для нас и компьютеров». Правильная связка = As for X…
maybe computer games will be activities for any teenagerscomputer games are a common hobby for many teenagersБудущее время не подходит — это общее правило (Present Simple). И «any teenagers» → «many».
My favorite way to spend free time is mobileMy favourite way to spend free time is playing mobile games«Mobile» само по себе = телефон/подвижный. Нужно «playing mobile games» / «watching videos on my phone».
it is my favorite sportit's my favourite hobbyМобильные игры — не спорт. Ошибка категории.
Отсутствует концовкаThat's all I wanted to say. Thanks for your attention.Обязательная OGE-frame. Без неё K2 = 1 балл максимум.

Точные формулировки уровней (для справки)

Письмо · К1 (макс 3): 3 = все аспекты раскрыты + 1 неполный допускается · 2 = 1 аспект не раскрыт ИЛИ 2-3 неполно · 1 = 2 не раскрыты ИЛИ 4 неполно · 0 = хуже.

Письмо · К3 (макс 3): 3 = 1 л-г ошибка · 2 = 2-3 · 1 = 4 · 0 = 5+.

Письмо · К4 (макс 2): 2 = 2 ошибки · 1 = 3-4 · 0 = 5+.

Monologue · К1 (макс 3): все 4 аспекта, 10-12 фраз, полно · К2 (макс 2): вступление + заключение обязательны · К3 (макс 2): 1-2 ошибки = 2 балла, 3-4 = 1, 5+ = 0.

Что тянем на следующей паре (15 мин точечно)

  1. Double-L drill: personally · usually · really · finally · actually · especially · generally — 10 слов на карточке.
  2. Апостроф-drill: haven't · don't · isn't · doesn't · won't — 10 предложений на слух.
  3. British English свод: favourite · colour · centre · realise · organise · theatre.
  4. Spend time + предлог: spend time on hobby · spend money on something.
  5. Slang → formal замена: chilling → relaxing at home / hanging out → spending time with friends / vibe → atmosphere.
  6. Sculpt vs sculpture и «to start + gerund»: to start sculpting · to start learning.
  7. Structural connectors для Task 3: First / Second / Also / To sum up — вставь минимум 2 в следующий монолог.
