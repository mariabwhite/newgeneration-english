## Murphy — что реально полезного для B2+ (эксклюзив)

### U1 · Present continuous
- Form: `am/is/are + V-ing`
- **Use A:** action happening right now at the moment of speaking («the water is boiling»)
- **Use B:** action around now, not at this moment («I am reading a book by Tolstoy»)
- **Use C:** changing situations (get, change, become, rise, fall, grow, improve, begin, start)

### U2 · Present simple
- Form: `he/she/it + V-s`
- **Use A:** things in general / true in general («nurses look after patients»)
- **Use B:** repeated action / habit («I get up at 7»)
- **Use C:** signal words — always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every N
- **Use D:** stage-direction present — I promise / I apologise / I insist / I suggest

### U3 · Cont vs Simple (1)
- Temporary vs general is the whole game
- «I’m living with friends until I find a flat» (temporary) vs «My parents live in London» (general)
- «always + V-ing» = criticism / annoying habit («you’re always losing your keys»)

### U4 · Stative verbs (NEVER continuous unless meaning shifts)
- like, love, hate, want, need, prefer, know, realise, suppose, mean, understand, believe, remember, belong, contain, consist, depend, seem
- **Dual-meaning tricks:**
  - «She is selfish» (character) ↔ «She is being selfish» (acting that way now)
  - think = opinion (simple) ↔ think = considering (continuous)
  - see/hear/smell/taste = simple present; use `can` («I can see», «I can hear»)
  - look / feel = BOTH forms possible

### U46 · Reported speech (1) — backshift
- am/is → was · are → were · do/does → did · will → would · can → could · must → had to
- want/like/know/go/think → wanted/liked/knew/went/thought
- **Keep present** if still true: «Tom said he has a new job» (still has it)

### U47 · Reported speech (2)
- **say/tell:** `tell + somebody`, `say` (no object) or `say to sb`
- **Imperatives:** `told/asked sb (not) to do` — «Drink plenty of water» → doctor told him to drink…
- **Questions:**
  - word order → statement
  - yes/no → `if / whether`
  - wh- → wh-word
  - backshift tense same as statement
