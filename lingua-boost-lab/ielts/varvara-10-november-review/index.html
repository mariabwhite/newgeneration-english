Task 1 · line chart (Band 7+ target)

The chart below shows the number of international postgraduate students enrolled in UK art-history programmes between 2010 and 2024. Summarise the main features and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.

Band 7 model opener (write your own first)

💡 Band 8 model (160 words)
Band 8 model · 162 words

The line chart illustrates how enrolment in UK art-history postgraduate programmes evolved across three categories of institution between 2010 and 2024.

Overall, all three groups saw growth, but online-only MAs rose dramatically from a standing start, whereas Russell-group figures grew steadily and non-Russell universities displayed the sharpest acceleration in the final period.

Looking at the Russell group, enrolment climbed consistently from 420 in 2010 to around 1 020 by 2024, though the pace eased noticeably after 2022. Non-Russell institutions followed a flatter trajectory until 2018, after which numbers jumped from 410 to 710 — an increase of roughly 73 % in just six years.

The most striking trend, however, was the rise of online-only MAs. Virtually non-existent in 2010 (20 students in 2014), they surged past 420 in 2022 and nearly doubled again by 2024, reaching 760 — a figure which now rivals established non-Russell enrolment.

Tip of the day · T1 structure
Paragraphs: intro paraphrase · overview (2 main features, no data!) · body 1 (data batch 1) · body 2 (data batch 2). Overview без цифр — это Band 7 threshold. Если overview = «there was a rise», -1 к Task Achievement.

Writing Task 2 · micro · opinion thesis + 1 counter-arg

8 min

Task 2 · agree or disagree

Some people believe university degrees in the arts and humanities are less valuable than those in science and technology. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Micro-sprint · не целое эссе · только thesis + 1 counter-arg

Formulate (1) your thesis statement (1 sentence, clear stance), (2) one strongest counter-argument against your position, (3) your rebuttal to that counter-argument. Это diagnoses C1 Argumentation — слабое место band 7→8.

Thesis
Counter-arg
Rebuttal
💡 Band 8 model skeleton
Band 8 skeleton · 85 words

Thesis: While STEM degrees undoubtedly carry stronger immediate earning power, I disagree that arts and humanities qualifications are intrinsically less valuable — their worth simply manifests on different timescales and across different domains.

Counter-argument: Critics typically point to graduate salary data: STEM graduates in the UK earn, on average, 30-40 % more within five years of graduation, suggesting the market itself has delivered a verdict on relative value.

Rebuttal: Yet this reasoning overlooks the fact that market pricing is a notoriously poor proxy for societal value — the fields that build cultural memory, legal reasoning and critical scrutiny are precisely those whose outputs resist monetisation.

Fresh November leak · reported 30.10.2026

10 min

Одна свежая карта из ноябрьского кластера Preptical / IELTSActualTests. Диагностика — переносится ли октябрьский каркас на новый prompt.

Describe a historical place or building you would like to visit.

You should say:

  • what this place or building is
  • where it is located
  • what you know about it
  • and explain why you would like to visit it.
⏱ 1 min prep · 2 min talk · идеально для твоей MA-темы (архитектура / археология)
Band 9 model · ~210 words · teacher voice

If I had to pick one historical place I'd genuinely love to visit, it would be the ancient city of Petra in southern Jordan — the Nabataean capital carved directly into rose-pink sandstone cliffs around the 4th century BCE.

What draws me to Petra isn't just that it's visually spectacular — though it certainly is. It's the fact that an entire city, including a thousand-seat theatre, royal tombs and an elaborate water-channel system, was quite literally cut out of the rock by a civilisation we still know surprisingly little about. For an archaeologist, that combination of monumental scale and biographical obscurity is worth its weight in gold.

What I'd most want to do is walk the Siq — the narrow, kilometre-long canyon that opens, almost theatrically, onto the façade of the Treasury. I've seen countless photographs, but I suspect no image can really do justice to the shift in light and scale when you emerge from that passage.

All things considered, Petra sits at the intersection of everything that drew me into this field: engineering ambition, cross-cultural trade, and a landscape that still holds unanswered questions.

Writing Task 2 · 3 November 2026 predictions

T2 · 1 · Preserving culture (agree/disagree)

«Every culture, throughout time, has recorded its history in one way or another. Some people say that it is important to preserve these records; others believe that we should move on and let the past go. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.»

💡 Thesis brainstorm: «While I accept there are limits to how much the past should dictate the present, on balance I strongly believe that preserving historical records remains essential — both as a safeguard against repeating past mistakes and as a foundation for cultural identity.»
T2 · 2 · Traditional ways of life (double-edged)

«With the development of modern society is the loss of traditional ways of life. Is it important to keep our traditional ways of life, or should we accept that this is natural progress?»

💡 Thesis brainstorm: «Modernisation is undoubtedly a double-edged sword: while it has raised living standards for billions, it has also eroded traditional ways of life that gave communities their identity. I will argue that both should coexist through conscious preservation efforts rather than being seen as mutually exclusive.»
T2 · 3 · E-books vs paper books (discuss both)

«Some people think that e-books are the death of paper books. To what extent do you agree or disagree?»

💡 Thesis brainstorm: «Although e-books have transformed how we consume written content — making entire libraries portable and affordable — I disagree that they will cause the extinction of paper books. Printed books offer a tactile experience, a cultural permanence and a form of ownership that digital files cannot replicate.»
