Sonya · 4 Present Tenses · Recognise the Form & Use It

Four tenses live in the present: Present Simple (habit), Present Continuous (right now), Present Perfect (result / experience) and Present Perfect Continuous (process / duration). Today we look at each shape, name it, use it, and check spelling of the small words that trip everyone up.

🕐 60 min 🔵 Present Simple 🟢 Present Continuous 💎 Present Perfect 🌊 Perfect Continuous ✏️ Spelling
01

The 4 shapes on one page

A2 → B1
🧭 The shape tells you the tense. Learn to see the shape first, then decide the meaning. 👀 Look for: -s / -es · am/is/are + -ing · have/has + V3 · have/has been + -ing.
Read each row aloud. Point at the shape column. Say the name of the tense.
Tense Shape (form) Example Signal words
🔵 Present SimpleV1 / V1-sSonya drinks tea every morning.always, usually, every day, on Mondays
🟢 Present Continuousam / is / are + V-ingSonya is drinking tea right now.right now, at the moment, look!
💎 Present Perfecthave / has + V3Sonya has already drunk her tea.already, just, yet, ever, never, since, this week
🌊 Present Perfect Continuoushave / has been + V-ingSonya has been drinking tea for an hour.for + duration, since + start, all morning, lately
02

One verb · four shapes · «to read»

A2 → B1
📚 Take one verb and put it into every present tense. This is the ear-training: your ear must recognise read / is reading / has read / has been reading without stopping to think. Muffin the cat can't read, but Sonya can. Watch her all day 👀.
🔵 PRESENT SIMPLE · habit
Every evening Sonya reads for 30 minutes.
shape: read + s (she/he/it)
🟢 PRESENT CONTINUOUS · right now
Look — Sonya is reading on the sofa!
shape: is + reading
💎 PRESENT PERFECT · result
Sonya has read three chapters today.
shape: has + read (V3 = read)
🌊 PERFECT CONTINUOUS · process
Sonya has been reading for an hour.
shape: has been + reading
🎯 Test yourself: cover the labels. Read the four sentences. Can you name each tense from the shape alone?

05

State verbs · «I like», not «I am liking»

A2 → B1
🚫 Some verbs describe a state, not an action. They hate the -ing form in most cases.
Feelings: like, love, hate, want, need, prefer · Thinking: know, believe, understand, remember, mean · Senses: see, hear, smell, taste, feel · Owning: have (own), belong, own, contain. ✅ I like chocolate. · ❌ I am liking chocolate.
✅ I have known Katya for years. · ❌ I have been knowing Katya.
👉 With state verbs, choose Present Simple or Present Perfect. Skip the -ing forms.
06

Spelling · he / she / it endings

A2
✏️ In Present Simple, he / she / it adds an ending to the verb. Three rules:
1️⃣ Most verbs → + s: read → reads · walk → walks · sleep → sleeps.
2️⃣ Verbs ending in -o, -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -z → + es: go → goes · do → does · watch → watches · wash → washes · miss → misses · fix → fixes.
3️⃣ Verbs ending in consonant + y → -y → -ies: cry → cries · try → tries · study → studies. (But play → plays because «ay» is vowel + y.) 🎯 Say aloud: «She watches, she does, she goes, she studies, she plays». Feel the endings.
💡 Tip: if you hear a «z» or «ez» sound at the end (like «wash-ez»), you probably need -es.
07

Spelling · silent letters in simple words

A2
🤫 English hides letters. You write them but you don't say them.
· Wednesday → «WENZ-day» (silent d)
· February → «FEB-yoo-a-ry» (first r often skipped, but write it!)
· friend → «frend» (silent i)
· know / knee / knife → silent k
· write / wrong / wrist → silent w
· hour / honest → silent h
· chocolate → three syllables → two: «CHOK-lit»
· vegetable → «VEJ-tuh-buhl» 🎯 Whisper each word first, then write it. Muscle memory beats spelling rules.
08

Small words you'll spell 100 times

A2
👨‍👩‍👧 Simple words are actually the hardest — everyone thinks they know them.
Family: mother, father, sister, brother, daughter (silent «gh»!), son, aunt, uncle, cousin, grandma, grandpa.
Colours: red, blue, green, yellow, white, black, purple, orange, pink, brown, grey.
Days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday. Capital letter! 🎯 Sonya's grandma wrote her a letter last winter and spelled «Wensday». Sonya laughed for a week 😄.
📝 In Workbook W9-W10 you'll fill in the missing letters in these small words.
09

Speaking · say one sentence in every tense

A2 → B1
🎤 Pick any verb (drink / read / play / do). Say four sentences — one in each present tense. Time yourself: under 30 seconds. 🔸 Simple: I usually drink tea after school.
🔸 Continuous: I am drinking water right now.
🔸 Perfect: I have already drunk two glasses today.
🔸 Perfect Continuous: I have been drinking water since morning.
🏆 If you can do this with 3 different verbs in a row, you've locked in the shapes.

W4

Exercise D · choose the right tense for the meaning

A2 → B1
Only one tense fits. Look at signal words and meaning (habit / now / result / duration).
1. Look at the window! It _____ outside.
«Look!» → happening now → Present Continuous.
2. The train to Moscow _____ every hour.
Timetable / regular habit → Present Simple.
3. Great news! I _____ my homework.
Result + «already» → Present Perfect.
4. I'm out of breath — I _____ for 20 minutes.
Visible result + duration → Perfect Continuous.
5. My grandma _____ in this house since 1980.
«since 1980» + action verb → Perfect Continuous (also «has lived» works, but Continuous stresses the whole span).
6. I _____ Katya since primary school.
«know» is a state verb → Present Perfect (never «-ing»). ✅ have known
W5

Exercise E · find the extra word in each line

A2 → B1
Every line has one word too many. Tap it. Often it's a wrong «is / do / have / been».
1.I am have finished my homework.
Right: I have finished my homework.
2.She do plays the piano every day.
Right: She plays the piano. (Present Simple 3rd person = plays, no «do»)
3.Sonya has been read four books this summer.
Right: Sonya has read four books. (count → Perfect, not Continuous)
4.Muffin is has been sleeping all afternoon.
Right: Muffin has been sleeping. (no «is»)
5.Right now I have am doing my English homework.
Right: I am doing my homework. (right now → Continuous)
6.Have you did ever tried Japanese food?
Right: Have you ever tried? (no «did» with «have»)
7.Katya are is my best friend.
Right: Katya is my best friend. (single subject → «is»)
8.I have never been knew a person like her.
Right: I have never known. (know = state → Perfect, not Continuous, and «knew» is Past Simple; use «known»)

Exercise L · vocab focus · «because»

A2
📖 because (bɪˈkɒz) — for the reason that; RU: потому что. Common misspellings: becouse, becaus, because. Correct: b-e-c-a-u-s-e. Also common trap: «because» is followed by a subject + verb; «because of» is followed by a noun. 🔸 I stayed home because I was tired. · 🔸 I stayed home because of the rain.
1.Spell the word for «потому что» (7 letters):
2.Sonya is happy Katya called her. (reason + full clause)
3.We didn't go out the rain. (2 words · reason + noun)
4.I'm tired I have been studying all day. (full clause after)
5.Vasya is grumpy his broken toy. (2 words · noun after)
6.Muffin is purring I gave him fish. (full clause)
🧩 Word class check: «because» is a conjunction (a small word that links two ideas). It's not a verb, not a noun, not an adjective. It just links.
