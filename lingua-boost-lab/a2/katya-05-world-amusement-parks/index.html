Safety words · rules on every ride

Every big roller coaster has these signs. Learn to read them — you'll see them at every park in the world.

⚠ Warning signs

  • MINIMUM HEIGHT · 130 cm
  • PREGNANT WOMEN — do not ride
  • NECK / BACK PROBLEMS — do not ride
  • NO LOOSE OBJECTS (phone, keys, coins)
  • KEEP HANDS AND FEET INSIDE at all times

🚨 Emergency vocab

  • safety harness — привязной ремень
  • emergency stop — экстренная остановка
  • evacuate — эвакуировать
  • breakdown — поломка
  • closed for repairs — закрыто на ремонт
  • ride at your own risk — катаетесь на свой страх и риск

🎢 World's most famous parks · curious & dangerous facts

Five real parks from four continents. Read each card, then answer the questions below. New vocab is in bold.

🇺🇸 Six Flags Great Adventure · USA

Kingda Ka — the tallest roller coaster in the world (139 m / 456 feet). It goes from 0 to 206 km/h in 3.5 seconds. Riders wear special safety harnesses. The ride only lasts 28 seconds, but the queue is often two hours long. It closed in 2024 — too many breakdowns.

🇦🇪 Ferrari World · UAE

Formula Rossa — the fastest roller coaster in the world (240 km/h). Faster than a Formula 1 car at launch. Riders must wear safety goggles because the wind is so strong. The launch is like an airplane taking off. Minimum height: 130 cm.

🇯🇵 Nagashima Spa Land · Japan

Steel Dragon 2000 — the longest roller coaster in the world (2.5 km). It has a big drop of 93 metres. In 2003 a wheel fell off during a ride — no one was hurt, but the ride was closed for many months for repairs. Now it is one of the safest in Japan.

🇺🇸 Action Park · USA (closed)

In New Jersey in the 1980s. Famous for the most dangerous water rides in history — a looping water slide that no one could finish, a wave pool nicknamed «The Grave Pool». More than 100 accidents. The park closed in 1996. Now there is a Netflix documentary about it.

🇺🇸 McKamey Manor · «the most extreme haunted house»

McKamey Manor in Tennessee is called the scariest haunted house in the world. The tour lasts 10 hours — actors chase you, scream, throw water. The owner offered $20,000 to anyone who finished the whole tour. Nobody has ever won. Visitors must sign a 40-page waiver and be over 21. Not for children!

Role-play · 3 mini scenes at a park

Practice with the teacher — two roles per scene. Then swap.

🎫 Scene 1 · At the ticket booth

Cashier: Good morning! How many tickets?

You: Two adults and one child, please.

Cashier: That will be sixty-five euros. Would you like a fast pass?

You: Yes, please. Two fast passes.

Cashier: That is one hundred and five euros total. Card or cash?

You: Card, please. Contactless.

Cashier: Enjoy your day at the park!

🆘 Scene 2 · Lost in the park

You: Excuse me, I can't find my friend. Where is the information desk?

Guide: Go straight, past the ferris wheel, then turn left. It's near the food court.

You: Thank you! And where is the exit?

Guide: The main exit is behind the parade square. Follow the yellow arrows.

You: Great, thanks a lot!

🎢 Scene 3 · Choosing a ride

Friend A: Let's go on the biggest roller coaster!

Friend B: No way, I'm scared. Can we do the carousel first?

Friend A: Ok, but after that we do the drop tower.

Friend B: Is it very high?

Friend A: About 60 metres. You'll scream, but you'll love it!

Friend B: Fine — but you go first!
