Hôtel Léonie · hotel essentials + Present Simple vs Continuous

15 min · 22 phrases · 4 drills · stative-verb trap
Quick recall · the new grammar

Routine or right-now? — 3-second test

Present Simple: I work · she works. Signal: every day · usually · often · on Mondays · twice a week. For routines, facts, timetables.

Present Continuous: am/is/are + V-ing. Signal: right now · at the moment · this week · today · still. For right-now and this-week.

Stative verbs — only PS: know · like · love · want · need · have (= own) · understand · remember. ❌ I'm knowing Paris. → ✅ I know Paris.

-ing traps: take → taking, stop → stopping, lie → lying.
