Rule · Present Simple

Routines · permanent facts · timetables

Use Present Simple when something happens every day, is generally true, or belongs to a timetable.

PersonPositiveNegativeQuestion
I / you / we / theyI workI don't workDo you work?
he / she / itshe worksshe doesn't workDoes she work?

Signal words: every day · usually · often · sometimes · never · on Mondays · twice a week

Examples (hotel):

  • Breakfast is from 7 to 10. (timetable)
  • I travel for work twice a year. (routine)
  • This hotel has free Wi-Fi. (fact)

Grammar · Present Continuous · the new piece

THE core rule for this lesson
Rule · Present Continuous

Right now · around now · this week · changing situation

Form: am / is / are + V-ing

PersonPositiveNegativeQuestion
II'm readingI'm not readingAm I reading?
he / she / itshe's workingshe isn't workingIs she working?
we / you / theywe're waitingwe aren't waitingAre we waiting?

Signal words: right now · at the moment · currently · this week · today · still

-ing spelling traps:

  • work → working (just add -ing)
  • take → taking (drop silent -e)
  • stop → stopping (double final consonant after short vowel: stop, run, swim, sit)
  • lie → lying (-ie → -ying)

Stative verbs — don't use in PC: know · like · love · hate · want · need · understand · believe · remember · own · have (= own). Say I know Paris, not I'm knowing Paris.

Drill PC-1 · fill the Present Continuous form
1. I ______ the menu right now. (read)
2. Marco ______ a call at the moment. (finish)
3. We ______ at Hôtel Léonie this week. (stay)
4. It ______ outside — we can walk to the Louvre. (not rain)
5. I ______ at the brasserie right across the hotel. (sit)
6. Marco and Sasha ______ tomorrow's trip to the Marais. (plan)

PS vs PC · how to choose in 3 seconds

Decision tree + the signal words that always help
Rule · side-by-side

Routine or right-now?

CluePresent SimplePresent Continuous
When?every day · in general · always trueright now · around today · this week
Signalusually / often / every / on Mondaysright now / at the moment / today / this week
ShapeI work / she worksI'm working / she's working
ExampleI live in Moscow.I 'm staying in Paris this week.
ExampleMarco works in Rome.Marco 's finishing a call right now.
Trapstative verbs stay here (know, like, want)never with stative verbs

3-second decision: ask yourself «around now / changing?» — yes → PC. «always / in general?» — yes → PS.

Speaking · 4 role-plays

Say each one out loud · teacher checks pronunciation
Role-play 1 · Hotel check-in (you = Sasha)

Reception at Hôtel Léonie

Walk up to the desk. In four sentences: greet, give your name, confirm nights, ask about breakfast. Use at least one Present Simple ("Breakfast is included?") and one Present Continuous ("I 'm staying three nights").

Role-play 2 · Café small-talk with Marco

"How is your week going?"

Marco asks «What are you doing this week?». Answer with: 1 PS routine ("I usually…") + 1 PC right-now ("Right now I…") + 1 PC this-week ("This week I…"). 3 sentences · 20 seconds.

Role-play 3 · Describing a photo

Tell me what is happening in the picture

Imagine you show Nastya a photo of the café. Use only Present Continuous to describe at least four actions you see: Marco is talking / The waiter is bringing / People are walking / The sun is going down.

Role-play 4 · The call back to Nastya

Phone call home · 30-45 seconds

Later that evening · Pont des Arts · the call home

Call Nastya. Start with the softener: Hey, do you have a minute? Then give her: where you are now (PC) + what you usually do on holiday (PS) + what you are doing tomorrow (PC + «going to» from L3). End with: «Talk later, I'll send pictures.»
