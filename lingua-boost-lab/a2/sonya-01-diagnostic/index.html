Doing right now vs doing usually

6 items
Usually, every day, always — verb without -ing. I read books every evening. · She drinks coffee in the morning. Right now, at this moment — verb + -ing with am / is / are before it. I am reading a book right now. · She is drinking coffee.

05

Articles: a / an / the / no article

6 items
a / an — «one of many, mentioned for the first time». I saw a cat in the yard. · She wants an apple. the — «this exact one, we already know it» or «the only one». The cat was black. · The sun is bright today. no article (—) — with names of cities, countries (except the US, the UK), languages, most sports. — I live in Moscow. — She speaks Spanish. — We play tennis.

06

Build a question from the words

4 sentences
An English question almost always has a small helper at the front: do / does / did / is / are / was / were / have / will. Usual order: (what?) → helper → who → verb → the rest? Where do you live? · What time does she get up? · Have you finished your homework? · What are you doing?

Tap a word in the bank — it moves into the sentence. Tap a word in the sentence — it returns.
