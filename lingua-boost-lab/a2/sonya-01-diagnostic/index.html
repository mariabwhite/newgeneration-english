01

to be · am / is / are

A1
am — with I. is — with he / she / it. are — with you / we / they.I am a student. · She is my sister. · They are at home.
Fill in am / is / are:
1.I a student.
2.My best friend from Kazan.
3.My parents at work now.
4.She at home — she is at the gym.
5.— you tired? — Yes, a little.
02

to have · possessive 's

A1
I / you / we / they have. He / she / it has. Possessive 's = «X's Y» (of X).I have a dog. · She has three books. · My sister's room is bigger than mine.
Fill in have / has, and the correct possessive form:
1.I two brothers.
2.My mum a small car.
3.I a pet.
4.This is my jacket. (brother)
5.My house is in the countryside. (parents)
03

Present Simple · find the mistake

A1
3rd person (he/she/it) gets -s. In questions and «not» we use do / does — the main verb stays in its base form.She works. · Does she work? · She doesn't work.
Tap the WRONG word in each sentence:
1.She get up at 7.
Right: She gets up at 7.
2.Where do you lives?
Right: Where do you live?
3.He don't like coffee.
Right: He doesn't like coffee.
4.My sister watch films every evening.
Right: My sister watches films every evening.
5.Does your friends speak English?
Right: Do your friends speak English?

05

Usually vs right now

A2
Usually / every day / always → simple form. Right now / at the moment → -ing form.She usually walks to school. · She is walking to school now.
1.My dad ___ coffee every morning.
2.Please be quiet. The baby ___.
3.I ___ TV right now — I'm reading.
4.___ tennis on weekends?
5.Sonya ___ fast food.

07

will vs going to

A2
going to = planned in advance, already decided. will = deciding right now, or a promise.I'm going to visit my aunt on Sunday (planned). · The phone's ringing — I'll get it (deciding now).
1.Next summer I ___ my grandma — we booked the tickets last week.
2.— This bag is heavy. — I ___ you.
3.Look at the sky — it ___.
4.I ___ you when I arrive. Promise.
5.What ___ after school today?
08

Present Perfect · never / just / already / yet

B1
have / has + 3rd form of the verb. Small helpers: never (in life), just (a moment ago), already (earlier than expected), yet (used in ? and — sentences at the end).I have never been to Paris. · She has just called. · I have already finished. · Have you eaten yet?
Fill in the missing word (never / just / already / yet):
1.I have tried sushi.
2.She has arrived — she is at the door.
3.Have you finished your homework ?
4.I have seen this film — twice, actually.
09

If it rains, I will…

B1
Real future condition: after if — simple form (no «will»). In the main part — will.If it rains, I will stay home. · She will pass if she studies.
1.If it tomorrow, we'll stay home. (rain)
2.If you don't sleep enough, you tired. (be)
3.She will pass the exam if she hard. (study)
4.If I have time, I you tomorrow. (call)

A2

A2
Things you can count (books, apples, cars) → many / a few. Things you can't count (sugar, water, information) → much / a little. a lot of works with both.How many apples? · How much water? · a lot of friends / a lot of time.

14

can · must · should

A2
can = ability / permission (I can swim; Can I come in?). must = strong obligation (You must wear a seatbelt). should = advice, «it's a good idea» (You should try it).I can play the guitar. · You must stop at a red light. · You should drink more water.
Read the situation → tap what you'd say:
Your friend asks if you know how to ride a bike.
Ability → can.
Your friend is driving without a seatbelt. You want to say it's the law.
Strong rule / law → must.
Your friend has a headache. You give friendly advice.
Advice → should.
You're at a friend's house and want to use the bathroom. Ask politely.
Permission → Can I…?
Your friend is eating a lot of chocolate before bed. Kind advice.
Kind advice, not a strict ban → shouldn't.

📖 R

Reading · Sonya's morning

A2

My name is Sonya. I'm fourteen. I live in Moscow with my mum, dad and little brother Max. On school days I usually get up at 7 a.m., but I don't like mornings. My mum always makes breakfast for us — she is a great cook.

I go to a German school, so I study German every day. I also speak a little Spanish. English I learn mostly from films and music. My favourite subject is history — the teacher is really kind and knows a lot of stories.

After school I usually meet my friends in a café next to the school. Sometimes we go to the cinema on Sundays. Last weekend I went to see a new film with my best friend Alice — it was funny.

True or False:
1.Sonya's mum cooks breakfast every day.
2.Sonya learns English at school as a subject.
3.Sonya likes her history teacher.
4.Last weekend Sonya went to the cinema alone.

Listening · dinner talk

A2
Sonya and mum in the kitchen · Sunday evening
≈ 30 sec · slow · British

Mum: Sonya, dinner is almost ready. Can you set the table, please?

Sonya: Sure. How many plates? Just us three?

Mum: Four — Dad is coming home in ten minutes. And bring the salt, it's on the top shelf.

Sonya: Do we have any bread?

Mum: Yes, there's some in the fridge. Take it out. And, Sonya — please don't forget your homework tonight, all right?

True or False (listen once, don't peek at the transcript):
1.Mum asks Sonya to set the table.
2.They will have dinner for three people.
3.The salt is on the top shelf.
4.There is bread in the fridge.
5.Mum reminds Sonya about her homework.
