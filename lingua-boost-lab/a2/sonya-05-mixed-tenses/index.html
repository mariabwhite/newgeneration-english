Sonya · Mixed Tenses · Perfect, Perfect Continuous & Past

Four powerful tenses meet on one page: Present Perfect (result), Present Perfect Continuous (process), Past Simple (finished moment) and Past Continuous (background scene). Today we see each shape, feel each meaning, and choose fast.

🕐 60 min 💎 Present Perfect 🌊 Perfect Continuous 🟠 Past Simple 🟡 Past Continuous 🎯 Mix & choose
01

Four tenses on one timeline

A2 → B1
🧭 Two tenses live in the present zone (they touch «now»), two tenses live in the past zone (they stay in «then»). Learn to see the shape first. 💎 Present Perfect and 🌊 Perfect Continuous → something touches NOW. 🟠 Past Simple and 🟡 Past Continuous → fully in the past.

03

Timeline · where each tense lives

A2 → B1
🗺️ Picture a line. On the right end — NOW. On the left end — then / earlier.
· Present Perfect sits between a past point and NOW: «done = still relevant».
· Perfect Continuous shows a long dashed line ending at NOW: «still going on».
· Past Simple is a dot in the past: closed, done, over.
· Past Continuous is a line in the past: a scene, a background.
⌛ The 4 tenses mapped onto a timeline
💎 Present Perfect
… ———✓→ 🕐 NOW
from past to now · result
🌊 Perfect Continuous
… ~~~~~~→ 🕐 NOW

🟠 Past Simple
… 🔘 ·…· 🕐 NOW

🟡 Past Continuous
… ~~~🔘~~~ ·…· 🕐 NOW

👉 In every workbook sentence: first find the «time anchor» (yesterday / already / for an hour / at 7 p.m.) — that tells you which part of the timeline you're in.
04

💎 Present Perfect vs 🟠 Past Simple · the classic trap

A2 → B1
🪤 Russian uses the same past form for both. English does NOT.
· Present Perfect = the action is connected to now (result still here, or «in my life so far»).
· Past Simple = the action is finished AND I know when (yesterday / in 2020 / last Monday). ✅ I have lost my keys. (…and I still can't find them — NOW matters.)
✅ I lost my keys yesterday. (…closed, past moment, time known.)
❌ I have lost my keys yesterday. ← yesterday kills Present Perfect.
Signal💎 Present Perfect🟠 Past Simple
Time wordalready, just, yet, ever, never, so far, this weekyesterday, last night, ago, in 2023, when I was 10
QuestionHave you ever been to Paris?When did you go to Paris?
Focusresult right nowmoment in the past
ExampleSonya has done her homework. (it's ready!)Sonya did her homework after school. (then.)

05

🌊 Present Perfect Continuous · process & duration

A2 → B1
⏳ Shape: have / has been + V-ing. Used when something has been going on recently and may still be going on. 🔸 I have been studying English for three years. (I started three years ago, still studying today.)
🔸 Muffin has been sleeping all afternoon. (long process, probably still sleeping now.)
🔸 Sonya has been playing the piano since 4 p.m. (she started at 4, still playing.)
🚦 Signal words for Perfect Continuous
for + duration since + start point all day / all morning lately recently How long…?

🚫 Watch out · state verbs skip -ing

State verbs (know, like, love, want, understand, believe, hate) stay in Present Perfect, not Continuous. ✅ I have known Katya for ten years. · ❌ I have been knowing Katya for ten years.

🟠 Past Simple vs 🟡 Past Continuous · interruption

A2 → B1
🎬 Picture a scene from yesterday:
· The long thing in the background → Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing).
· The short thing that happens / interrupts → Past Simple. 🔸 While I was reading, Muffin jumped on my book.
🔸 When mum came home, we were watching cartoons.
🔸 At 7 p.m. yesterday I was eating dinner. (background of the moment.)

09

Speaking · four shapes, four short tasks

A2 → B1
🎤 Four different speaking micro-tasks. Each one targets ONE tense. Say your sentences out loud — don't just read.

🎯 Task A · Have you ever? (Present Perfect)

Ask Katya three «Have you ever…?» questions, then answer the same three for yourself.
💡 Format: Have you ever + V3 …? → Yes, I have / No, I haven't. Examples: Have you ever tried sushi? Have you ever been to Istanbul? Have you ever broken your phone?

🎯 Task B · How long have you been…? (Perfect Continuous)

Pick 3 hobbies / habits (reading, piano, dance, English, drawing) and tell Katya how long you have been doing them.
💡 Format: I have been + V-ing + for / since …. Example: I have been dancing since I was five. I have been learning English for three years.

🎯 Task C · Yesterday timeline (Past Simple)

Tell Katya 5 things you DID yesterday — one sentence per thing. Use time words: in the morning, at 8 a.m., after school, in the evening, before bed.
💡 Format: Yesterday I … + V2. Example: Yesterday after school I did my homework and then I watched a cartoon.

🎯 Task D · The 7 p.m. scene (Past Continuous)

What were you doing at 7 p.m. yesterday? Describe the SCENE (5 details): where you were, what you were wearing, what you were doing, who was with you, what the room smelled like.
💡 Format: I was + V-ing. Example: At 7 p.m. I was sitting on my bed. I was wearing pyjamas. Muffin was sleeping next to me. Mum was cooking in the kitchen.

Звук «j» пишется как «g» перед «e». То же слово, что и фрукт.
7. Брюки:
t-r-o-u-s-e-r-s. Всегда во множественном числе (как ножницы).
8. Свитер (UK):
j-U-m-p-e-r (как jump + er). Одна «p».
9. Куртка:
j-a-c-k-e-t. Звук «к» = «ck» после короткой гласной.
10. Жираф:
g-i-r-a-ff-e. Начинается с «g» (звук «дж»), двойная «f», тихая «e».
11. Слон:
Звук «ф» пишется как «ph», не «f». Как в phone, photo.
12. Белка:
Самое страшное слово курса: s-q-u-i-rr-e-l. Двойная r.
13. Украшения (UK):
j-e-w-e-ll-e-r-y. UK вариант. US — jewelry, короче.
14. Ожерелье:
neck + lace. Произносим «NECK-lis», пишем neck-l-a-c-e.
15. Браслет:
b-r-a-c-e-l-e-t. Звук «с» = «c» перед «e».
