Chapter 1 · Our flat

Hi, I'm Julia and this is my family. We live in a nice flat in our city, not far from the big park where they have the fair every summer. There are three of us: my mum, my little brother John, and me. We've also got one very special family member — Barsik, our big grey cat. Barsik is the king of the flat. Everybody knows it.

Chapter 2 · Mum

My mum works at home. She has got long wavy brown hair and warm eyes. On school days she always drives John and me to school in our car — she doesn't like it when we walk alone, because the roads here are busy.

On Sundays Mum makes big pancakes with honey — the best pancakes in the world. We eat them with tea and raspberry jam. Then sometimes we all go for a walk in the city park.

Chapter 3 · My little brother John

John is my little brother. He is 6 years old and this is his first year at school — 1st grade. He loves horses, carousels and ice cream. Every morning John loses one of his shoes before school, and Mum shouts "Where is your shoe?!" and we all look for it. (Usually it is under the sofa. Always.)

Last Sunday Mum gave me 300 roubles and we went to the big fair with Emma. John rode a white carousel horse and said he was a cowboy. He put his orange ticket in his pocket and kept it like a treasure.

Chapter 4 · Barsik the king

Barsik is a big grey cat with golden-green eyes and a long serious tail. He is 7 years old. He sleeps a lot, eats a lot, and watches everything that happens in the flat.

Barsik does NOT like small kittens. Last summer at the fair we met Mrs. Pilsberry, a cat breeder. She had six tiny Scottish Fold kittens, each for about 50 000 roubles. John wanted one so much! But I told him: "John, we already have Barsik at home. And Barsik is a king. He wouldn't like a tiny kitten in his kingdom." John agreed. Barsik agreed. Mum laughed.

(By the way — last Sunday at Aunt Anya's flat, her huge orange cat Rizhik jumped on the table and stole MY sausage. Chaos. Just so you know what a REAL troublemaker cat looks like.)

I love my little family.

2.4 Breakfast dialogue · Sunday morning in the kitchen

💬 Classic Monday morning in Julia's flat — John has lost one shoe (again), Mum is at the door with the pancakes, Barsik is watching from the window sill.

Click ▶ on each turn to hear the voice.

Mum: Good morning, everybody! The pancakes are ready. John — where is your other shoe?
John: I don't know, Mum… Maybe Barsik took it!
Julia: It's not Barsik. Barsik is sitting on the window sill — he is washing his paw.
Mum: Oh dear. John, come to the table, we have ten minutes before school. Can I have your plate, please?
Julia: Mum, I will find the shoe. (looks under the sofa) …Got it! Here, John!
John: YAY! Thank you, Julia! You are the best big sister!
Mum: Good team. And this evening we are going to call Grandma — don't forget, OK?

🔎 Did you notice? 3 tenses in one little talk

· Pancakes are ready → Present Simple (now, a fact)
· Barsik is sitting → Present Continuous (right at this moment)
· Maybe Barsik took it → Past Simple (finished action)
· we are going to call Grandma → going to (plan for later)
· I will tell her → will (decision right now)

Theory corner · will OR going to?

⚖ Compare
Two future tenses — what's the difference? One line, one example, side by side.
WILL prediction / promise / decision NOW
  • You THINK something will happen (guess).
    I think it will rain tomorrow.
  • You PROMISE right now.
    I will help you, Mum!
  • You decide at this very second.
    — I'm thirsty. — I'll bring you water!
GOING TO plan already in your head
  • You already DECIDED earlier.
    We are going to buy a present for Mum.
  • You SEE something and know what will happen.
    Look at those clouds — it's going to rain!
  • A plan with a time and a place.
    On Friday I'm going to bake a cake.
⚖ 🤔
