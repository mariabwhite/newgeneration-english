Have got + 's — who belongs to whom

10 min · theory · 2 drills · 1 builder
Theory · 2 min

have got — "у меня есть"

In British English, have got means «у меня есть». It's shorter than I have.

  • I have got a sister. (I've got)
  • You have got a brother. (You've got)
  • He / she / it has got a pet. (He's got / She's got)
  • We / they have got a big family. (We've got / They've got)

Rule: only he / she / it uses has. Everyone else uses have.

Negative: I haven't got a cousin. She hasn't got a brother.

Theory · 1 min

's — "чей / чья"

To say "whose", add 's to the person's name or role:

  • Wendy's mother = мама Венди
  • Dracula's castle = замок Дракулы
  • my brother's pet = питомец моего брата

Common mistake: The mother of Wendy — wrong. Say Wendy's mother.

On top of Pumpkin Hill, in a big dark castle, lives the Boo family. The father is Dracula. He is a vampire. He sleeps all day and works all night. The mother is Morticia. She is a witch and she has a big black cat.

Dracula and Morticia have got two children. Their daughter is Wendy. She is thirteen, like a human girl, but she is a friendly ghost. Wendy's little brother is Freddy. He is ten and he is a slow, hungry zombie. He loves pizza.

Speaking · Your turn

6 min · 5 prompts · answer out loud · use have got + 's
Theory · 30 sec

How to answer · 3 safe frames

Say every answer out loud first. Pattern templates:

  • I have got a sister / brother. or I haven't got a sister.
  • My sister's name is Anna. She is 8.
  • My family pet is a cat. Her name is …
Prompts · for Zhenya

Tell about YOUR family — not the Boo family

1. How many people are there in your family? Use There are …
2. Have you got a brother or a sister? Say name + age.
3. Who is your favourite person in your family? Why?
4. Have you got a pet? What's its name?
5. If your family was a monster family — who would be what monster? (My mum would be a …)
