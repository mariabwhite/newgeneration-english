Timing · how soon? when exactly?

7 min · time distance · time zones · 6 new phrases
Theory · new today

Three patterns · don't mix them up

Time distance (how much later from now): in two hours · in fifteen minutes — always in + amount.

Exact clock time: at six · at half past four · at nine pm — always at + clock.

Across time zones: add the city — at six Moscow time · at four Paris time. Without a city you can say at four local time.

Edges of the day: early in the morning · late at night / till late · after nine pm.

Polite softeners · five phrases you'll overuse

4 min · situation ↔ softener match
Theory · new today

Softeners make you sound native-smooth

Native speakers rarely say «yes» or «no» alone — they cushion it with a short phrase:

  • If possible — add it to any ask you aren't sure about.
  • That would be great — enthusiastic yes.
  • No problem — relaxed OK after someone apologises or asks a favour.
  • Sorry about that — tiny apology.
  • I appreciate it — warm thanks that isn't just «thank you».

Homework · before next lesson

Four short tasks, about 20 minutes

  1. WhatsApp chat — write a 6-message WhatsApp exchange with a colleague who wants a call. You must use at least 4 orange phrases, including I'm busy right now, What time are you free?, at six Moscow time, That would be great. Send it on Telegram.
  2. Voice note · 60 seconds — tell the story of today's phone call with Marco. Use at least 5 orange phrases. Hint: start with I was told to reach out to you or My friend recommended you.
  3. «Going to» plans · 5 sentences — write 5 sentences about your real plans for the next 24 hours. Pattern: I'm going to + verb. Example: I'm going to get a SIM card tomorrow.
  4. Reschedule role-play — write 8 questions a boss would ask you about availability. 4 must use What time…? / When…? / Could you…?, 4 must include an orange phrase in the ANSWER you'd give.

Bonus: next time you have to schedule something with someone — do it in English out loud (even to yourself). Report back next lesson how it felt.
