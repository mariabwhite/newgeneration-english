Present Simple · daily routine · the whole picture

3 · theory + positive/negative/question drills · odd-one-out
Theory · Present Simple

The three faces of Present Simple

What for? Routine (every morning), facts (water boils at 100 °C), schedules (the train leaves at seven). Not what's happening right now — that's Present Continuous.

Positive form:

  • I / you / we / they → drink, live, work (no change)
  • he / she / it → add -s: drinks, lives, works
  • Spelling: verbs in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o → add -es: goes, does, watches, teaches, washes
  • Verbs in consonant + y → change y → ies: study → studies, fly → flies

Negative form:

  • I / you / we / they → don't + verb: I don't drink coffee.
  • he / she / it → doesn't + verb (verb loses -s!): She doesn't drink coffee.

Question form:

  • I / you / we / they → Do + subject + verb?: Do you drink coffee?
  • he / she / it → Does + subject + verb?: Does she drink coffee?
  • Short answers: Yes, I do. / No, I don't. / Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

Signal words (triggers): every day · usually · often · sometimes · never · on Mondays · in the morning · twice a week.

Present Simple · во всей красе · на сегодняшнем vocab

12 min · theory + positive / negative / question · 4 drill blocks
Theory · Present Simple

Три формы · на фразах из сегодняшнего урока

Зачем нужен Present Simple? Routine (I work in marketing), facts (Paris is two hours behind Moscow), fixed states (I'm busy right now, I'm free from three to six). Это НЕ про «прямо сейчас в этот момент» — для действия в процессе есть Present Continuous.

① Positive form

  • I / you / we / they → глагол без изменений: I have a meeting at eight · I live in Paris · We work in marketing
  • he / she / it → глагол + -s: She has a meeting · Marco lives in Milan · The café opens at seven
  • Spelling spike: have → has, go → goes, do → does, watch → watches, fly → flies

② Negative form

  • I / you / we / they → don't + verb: I don't have mobile data yet · I don't work on Fridays
  • he / she / it → doesn't + verb (глагол теряет -s!): She doesn't have a French SIM · Marco doesn't live in Paris

③ Question form

  • Yes/No: Do/Does + subject + verb? → Do you have Wi-Fi here? · Does Marco work on weekends?
  • WH-: What/When/Where/How + do/does + subject + verb? → What time are you free? (be → are, no do needed) · When does the shop open?
  • Short answers: Yes, I do. / No, I don't. / Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

⚠️ Common traps

  • Does he lives in Milan? → Does he live in Milan? (один -s на предложение)
  • She don't have data. → She doesn't have data. (she/he/it → doesn't)
  • I'm have a meeting. → I have a meeting. (be не нужно с другими глаголами)

Signal words: every day · usually · often · sometimes · never · on Mondays · in the morning · twice a week.

Questions · special & general · WH-word drill

8 min · theory · 8 build-the-question drills · 10 pick-the-WH-word gap-fill
Theory · new today

Two kinds of English questions

Every English question is one of two shapes.

  • GENERAL (yes/no) — answered «yes» or «no». Start with a helper verb: Do / Does / Did / Is / Are / Was / Were / Can / Will.
    Do you have Wi-Fi here? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
    Is Marco busy right now? → Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
  • SPECIAL (WH-) — ask for information. Start with a WH-word, then the helper verb.
    What time is the meeting? When can we talk? Where are you right now?

Formula: WH-word + helper + subject + main verb + …?  →  What do you want to drink?

The six core WH-words
WH-word Что спрашивает Пример
Whatчто · какойWhat is your name? / What time is it?
Whereгде · кудаWhere are you right now?
WhenкогдаWhen can we talk?
Whyпочему · зачемWhy do you need Wi-Fi?
Whoкто · комуWho is calling you?
Howкак · насколько · сколькоHow long is the meeting? / How are you?
