02 · Gap-fill

be going to — future plans

Fill in one word per gap.

be going to + verb = plan or intention for the near future.
Structure: I am going to + verb. She is going to travel. They are going to call. ✔ I am going to call my mother.   ✘ I go call. ✘ I going call.
1. I going to call my mother.
2. She is going ask her how she is.
3. We are to visit our team.
4. He going to have a business trip.
5. They are going to some research.
03 · Fix the mistake

Rewrite these sentences correctly

Type the full corrected sentence.

Common A2 mistakes:
• With feelings, don't forget am / is / are.
• be allergic to + noun (add the for specific things like the sun).
• For a past finished trip use had, not have.
I allergic to sun.
Correct: I am allergic to the sun.
I hungry.
Correct: I am hungry.
I have business trip last week.
Correct: I had a business trip last week.
I go call my mom now.
Correct: I am going to call my mom now.
