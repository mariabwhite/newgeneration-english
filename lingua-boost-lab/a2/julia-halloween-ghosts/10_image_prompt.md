# 10 Halloween-ghost images · prompt for GPT-4o Images / Flux / Midjourney

Готовый master-prompt + 10 сцен — скопируй в чат, попроси "generate image 1 ... image 10" по одной, или пропусти через batch.

## MASTER STYLE (apply to every image)

> Soft cute watercolor + pencil-outline children's book illustration. Halloween night but friendly, not scary. Palette: pumpkin orange `#FF9138`, candy glow `#FFC97A`, ghost lavender `#C9A4F0`, candy pink `#FF8FC5`, warm parchment `#FBECD4`, deep plum night sky `#28163A`. Full big orange moon, little bats, swirling fog, floating fireflies. 3:2 landscape, printable (300dpi). No text, no watermark.

## CAST (consistent across all 10)

- **JULIA** — 10-year-old Russian girl, long chestnut-brown hair, big brown eyes, warm smile. **Costume**: WITCH — long black dress with little stars, tall purple-black pointy hat with orange ribbon, small broom.
- **JOHN** — Julia's little brother, 7-8 years old, short dark hair. **Costume**: VAMPIRE — black cape with red lining, pale white face, plastic fangs, slicked hair.
- **MAMA** — warm friendly mother in her 30s, chin-length dark hair. **Costume**: BLACK CAT — all-black outfit, cat-ears headband, drawn-on whiskers, tail.
- **PAPA** — tall friendly father in his 30s, kind eyes. **Costume**: MUMMY — wrapped head-to-toe in white toilet-paper strips, one strip loose and dragging.
- **EMMA** — Julia's best friend, 10 years old, blonde curly hair, freckles, cheerful. **Costume**: FRIENDLY GHOST — oversized white bedsheet with two round black eye-holes and a cute smile cut out, pink slippers peek out below.

## THE CASTLE

Old small European gothic castle on a hill at the end of Garden Street. Two round towers with pointy roofs, broken shutters, ivy on the walls, iron gate half-open, cobweb on the gate, big carved jack-o'-lantern near the entrance. Inside: wide stone hallway, grand staircase, crooked candles everywhere, old family portraits (one portrait winks). Feel: a little spooky but welcoming.

## THE FOUR FRIENDLY GHOSTS (appear in #6–#9)

- **GRANDMA GHOST** — kind old lady, translucent lavender-white, grey hair in a bun, apron, holding a lantern.
- **BOY GHOST** — chubby cheerful ghost boy ~8, floating, chocolate cookie in hand.
- **DOG GHOST** — big fluffy translucent sheepdog ghost, wagging tail, pink tongue.
- **WIZARD GHOST** — tall thin old wizard in a long star-covered robe, pointy hat, long white beard, wand with sparkle.

## 10 SCENES

**#1 · "The street at sunset"** — Julia, John, Mama, Papa, Emma on cobblestone Garden Street at dusk. Little cottages with pumpkins on porches. Everyone laughing, Julia holds a candy bag, John shows his fake fangs, Emma peeks through her ghost-sheet eye-holes. Warm orange sky with first stars.

**#2 · "First house — trick or treat"** — All five on a cottage porch. A kind grandmother drops candy from a bowl into Julia's bag. Three jack-o'-lanterns glow on the porch. John's bag already full. Mama laughing.

**#3 · "The castle at the end of the street"** — Wide shot: all five stand in front of the dark old CASTLE at the end of Garden Street. Huge mysterious silhouette against the full orange moon. Dead garden, iron gate ajar. Emma nervous, Julia determined, John excited.

**#4 · "Knocking at the castle door"** — Close-up of massive wooden castle door. John raises a small fist to knock. Julia, Emma, Mama, Papa behind him. A big spider sits on the door-knocker. Moonlight through fog.

**#5 · "The door opens by itself"** — Huge door slowly opening on its own, hinges glowing. Inside: dim orange glow with hundreds of candles. The family stands at the threshold, mouths open. Emma hides half behind Julia.

**#6 · "The grand staircase + Grandma Ghost"** — Castle hall with a grand curving stone staircase. On the stairs floats the GRANDMA GHOST — translucent lavender, lantern in hand, waving welcome. Julia smiles up. The others look up wide-eyed. Candles, dust in moonbeams.

**#7 · "Kitchen · Boy Ghost + candy"** — Castle kitchen with a huge fireplace. BOY GHOST floats near a wooden table, cookie in hand, smiling at John. Copper pans hanging. John offers him candy. Everyone watches and smiles. Cosy orange firelight.

**#8 · "Attic · Dog Ghost"** — Castle attic, wooden beams, old chests. DOG GHOST — big fluffy translucent sheepdog — wags his tail and licks Emma's face through the sheet. Emma giggles. Julia pets him. John and Papa laugh. One tiny bat on a beam. Moonlight through a round window.

**#9 · "Garden · Wizard Ghost · dancing"** — Overgrown castle garden at night. WIZARD GHOST, tall and translucent, waves his sparkling wand making stars twinkle. Papa (mummy) dances a funny dance with the wizard. Mama talks with the grandma ghost near a stone bench, holding a recipe scroll. Julia, John, Emma dance with the boy ghost around a pumpkin. Full orange moon, bats flying.

**#10 · "Running home with candy and magic"** — The five run down Garden Street toward their warm lit house in the distance. Candy bags bouncing. All happy, laughing, waving goodbye at the castle silhouette behind them. The four ghosts faintly appear in castle windows, waving back. Moon lower, sky deep lavender, almost morning.
