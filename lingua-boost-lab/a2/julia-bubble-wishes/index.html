2.1 Mum's secret bubble recipe

How to make giant bubbles — do these steps

1. Take a big bowl.
2. Pour 1 cup of water into the bowl.
3. Add 2 spoons of dish soap.
4. Mix gently with a spoon — don't shake!
5. Add 1 small spoon of glycerin (the magic ingredient).
6. Wait ten minutes. Then take your wand and blow!

👉 These are imperatives — you tell someone WHAT TO DO: Take · Pour · Add · Mix · Wait · Blow.

🔮 ⭐

3.1 Mini-theory · WILL for wishes and predictions

Formula

SubjectwillV (bare)
I / you / he / she / we / theywill (short: 'll)travel · see · meet · be happy
Use will for:
· a wish / dream about the future — I will travel to London one day.
· a prediction — I think it will rain tomorrow.
· a promise — I will always love Barsik.

Negative: won't (= will not). Question: Will you…? Short: Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

Chapter 3 · Barsik the bubble hunter

Past Continuous · flashback
Suddenly from inside — a grey missile. Barsik heard the pop and came hunting. Chaos ensues.
Chapter 3 · audio-first

Julia was blowing her tenth bubble when Barsik suddenly jumped onto the balcony table. He was running after a shiny pink bubble.

POP! The bubble burst on his nose. He stopped. He looked at his wet paw. He sneezed. Julia was laughing so hard she dropped the wand.

"BARSIK! That was my wish!" she said. "Now I will have to blow another one."

Barsik wasn't listening. He was already waiting for the next bubble, his tail moving left and right like a conductor's baton.

4.1 Mini-theory · Past Continuous + Past Simple together

· Past Continuous (was/were + V-ing) = a long action in the past
· Past Simple (V2) = a short action that cut it off

Ten minutes later, Emma rang the doorbell. In her hands was a huge wire ring, big as a plate.

They poured the mixture into a big bowl and took everything to the city park. The grass was warm. A soft wind was blowing.

🗓 📋

5.1 Mini-theory · will vs going to

WILLa wish, a prediction, a decision at this very momentI will help you! (right now I decide)
GOING TOa plan you ALREADY MADE beforeWe are going to make a giant bubble. (we planned it yesterday)
⚖ 🫧

🧩 🌧

5.3 Mini-theory · First conditional (If…, will…)

If + Present Simple, will + V (bare).
If I blow slowly, the bubble will be giant.
If it bursts, we will make another one.
⚠️ Never If I will blow… — only Present after if!

The sun was turning orange. Julia, John and Emma sat on the warm grass. The last bubble of the day floated slowly above the trees — bigger than any before.

"Maybe one day I could have my own art studio with a huge window," said Julia.

Barsik was already asleep in Julia's lap, dreaming his own bubble dreams.

6.1 Mini-theory · might / could — soft possibility

mightmaybe · 50/50 chanceI might become a scientist.
couldpossibility · gentler than canI could open an art studio.
After might / could — bare V (no to, no -s).
