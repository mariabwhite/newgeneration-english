Phrase bank

All the constructions you need to introduce yourself

Four buckets. Read each line out loud. Tick in your head the ones you already know.

🤝 Greeting & introductions

  • Hi. / Hello. — casual / neutral
  • Good morning / afternoon / evening. — formal (before 12 / 12–18 / after 18)
  • How are you? → I'm fine, thanks. And you?
  • My name is Georgiy. / I'm Georgiy.
  • You can call me Georgiy.
  • Nice to meet you. / Nice to meet you too.
  • Sorry, what's your name? / Could you say that again?

🌍 Nationality & where you live

  • I'm from Russia. — country, always with from
  • I live in Lisbon. / I live in Portugal now. — city/country, always with live in
  • I'm Russian. — nationality = adjective (not I'm Russia)
  • How about you? / And you? — bounce the question back

💼 Work

  • I'm a / an + job. — "I'm an engineer." · "I'm a teacher."
  • I work in + field. — "I work in IT." · "I work in marketing."
  • I work as a + role + at + company. — "I work as a manager at Yandex."
  • I work from home. / I work in an office.
  • I'm self-employed. / I'm retired. / I'm a student.
  • Do you like your job? → Yes, I like it a lot.

🏠 Daily life basics

  • I usually get up at seven. / I go to bed at eleven.
  • I have breakfast at home. / I grab a coffee on the way.
  • I take the bus / the metro.
  • In the evenings I watch films / series / play games.
  • On weekends I usually walk around Lisbon.
  • Yes, please. / No, thanks.
  • Can I pay by card? / Can I have a bag?

Scene 1 · The lift · a neighbour spots your Netflix hoodie · Lisbon
Neighbour

Hey, nice hoodie. Stranger Things fan?

You

Yeah, big fan. I'm halfway through season 4.

Neighbour

Oh, no spoilers then. I'm Pedro, by the way — flat 4B.

You

Georgiy — 4A. I just moved in last month.

Neighbour

Moscow originally. And you — are you from here?

I'll take you up on that. Thanks, Pedro.

Speak after Scene 1

Your turn · meet a neighbour in English

Imagine you meet a new neighbour in the lift. Pick one show you actually watch. Build 6 lines in your head, then say them out loud:

  1. Hi. I'm Georgiy — flat ___.
  2. I just moved in last ___.
  3. I'm from Moscow. And you?
  4. I'm into ___. Have you seen it?
  5. I'm on season ___ right now.
  6. Nice to meet you, ___.

Pattern of the day: I'm halfway through… · I'm into… · No spoilers!

Georgiy's Friday night

Georgiy is thirty-eight. He is from Moscow and he lives in Lisbon now. He works in IT. On Friday evening he usually orders food and watches a film at home. Tonight he is rewatching Breaking Bad with English subtitles — he thinks it helps his listening. His neighbour Pedro texts him: "Beer at the bar downstairs?" Georgiy pauses the show and goes down for one quick drink. They talk about Dune: Part Two for twenty minutes. Pedro recommends Better Call Saul. Georgiy adds it to his watchlist and goes back home to his couch.

12 min · three different formats
Two truths & a lie

Say three sentences about yourself — two true, one false

Template: I'm from ___. I work as a ___. I live in ___. Your teacher guesses the lie. Then swap.

  1. Plan 3 facts before you speak (2 real + 1 fake).
  2. Say all three in a flat tone — don't give away the lie with your voice.
  3. After the guess: You're right / You're wrong. The lie was ___.

Present Simple · the base you'll use every day

10 min · form · negatives · questions · travelling questions
Theory · form

Present Simple in one screen

Positive: I / you / we / they + verb · he / she / it + verb + s.

Negative: I / you / we / they + don't + verb · he / she / it + doesn't + verb.

Question: Do + I / you / we / they + verb? · Does + he / she / it + verb?

Use it for: habits, routines, facts. Signal words: every day / usually / often / sometimes / never.
