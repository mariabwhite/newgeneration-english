Section 04

a · the · nothing

Three rules only. a = one of many, first time. the = the exact one we know. — = things you cannot count (coffee, money, water, time). Tap the right choice for each blank.

Section 05

Six lines you actually say

Every line is a short, useful, adult phrase. Say each one out loud two times. Slow first, natural second.

🎙 read → speak · x2 each
☕ Coffee shop
Could I have a large flat white, please? To go, thanks.
Say could I have, not give me. Polite is normal here.
🏨 Hotel check-in
Hi, I have a reservation under the name Timofey. For two nights.
Use under the name, not on the name.
🤝 Meeting introduction
Nice to meet you. I work at Lukoil, in the strategy team.
Use at for company, in for department.
🕓 Ask to leave work
Would it be all right if I left early today? I have a family matter.
Formal but not stiff. Family matter = short and enough.
🚕 In a taxi
Could you drop me at the corner, please? Then I'll walk.
Drop me at — the natural way to say “stop the car and let me out here”.
📧 Short business email
Hi Marco, could you send me the updated file today? Thanks — Timofey.
Two lines is enough. Short is professional here.

Homework · practical · 4 short blocks

Five to seven minutes only. Slow and clear. If a block is too easy — good, that means it works.

A · Fix the small word (10 items)

1. I want ___ speak English better.
2. She has ___ finish this report today.
3. We need ___ book a hotel.
4. I'd like ___ have some tea.
5. He wants ___ visit Milan next year.
6. They need ___ send the file.
7. Can I ___ ask a question?
8. I have ___ leave in five minutes.
9. She'd like ___ take a break.
10. You need ___ relax more.

B · a / the / — (6 items)

1. I have ___ new task from the boss.
2. ___ task is not easy.
3. I drink ___ tea every evening.
4. Could I have ___ tea, please?
5. Open ___ window, please.
6. ___ time is money.

C · Speaking · your usual working day (offline)

Record a 60-second voice message. Answer this question: What do you do every day at work? Use five or six sentences. Slow pace. Send the audio to Maria.

D · Watch & catch (optional)

Pick one short YouTube video on any topic you like — maximum three minutes, native English. Write down three sentences you hear that use need to / want to / have to. Just three. Bring them to the next lesson.
For teacher · answer keys

A · to-infinitive: all answers = to. Exception: 7. Can I ask — no to after modal can.

B · articles: 1. a · 2. The · 3. — · 4. a · 5. the · 6. — (Time is money — proverb).
