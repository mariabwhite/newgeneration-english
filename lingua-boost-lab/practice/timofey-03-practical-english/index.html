Section 06b

Put the vocab to work

Three quick practice runs on the words you just met, then six mini-questions to say out loud.

A · Match word → meaning (tap to reveal)

1. deadline — what does it mean here?
✅ deadline = the point in time by which work must be done. I have a deadline on Friday.
2. reschedule — closest meaning?
✅ reschedule = change the time of a meeting or call. Can we reschedule to Thursday?
3. reservation — where is it used?
✅ reservation = a booking you made in advance. I have a reservation under Timofey.
4. invoice — what does it stand for?
✅ invoice = the document asking for payment. I sent the invoice yesterday.
5. hand over — best fit for the sentence?
✅ hand over = give control / a task to someone else. I'll hand over the file to Marco.
6. on time vs. in time — pick the natural one:
“I finished the report ___ (before the deadline, not late).”
✅ on time = punctual (right at the expected moment). In time = with time to spare before something bad. Deadline → on time.

Section 01b · Grammar All 4 present tenses on one page
Section 01b

All four Present tenses on one page

One line for each tense — shape, when to use it, and a real business sentence. Open, glance, keep going.

Tense Shape When to use Real sentence
Present Simple V / V-s routine · fact · every day · always / usually / sometimes I work at Lukoil. She checks emails every morning.
Present Continuous am/is/are + V-ing right now · around now · temporary · trend I'm working on the report right now. We're hiring three engineers this month.
Present Perfect have/has + V3 result now · life experience · just / already / ever / never / yet I've sent the invoice. She has already approved the budget.
Present Perfect Continuous have/has been + V-ing started in the past · still going · for / since · focus on the process I've been working on this deck for two hours. We've been waiting since Monday.
🧠 Quick test: which tense fits?
“Today I ______ (finish) three tasks.” → Present Perfect — result now.
“Right now Marco ______ (write) the email.” → Present Continuous — action right now.
“I ______ (learn) English for six months.” → Present Perfect Continuous — process, still going.
“She usually ______ (arrive) at nine.” → Present Simple — routine.

Section 05

Six lines you actually say

Every line is a short, useful, adult phrase. Say each one out loud two times. Slow first, natural second.

🎙 read → speak · x2 each
☕ Coffee shop
Could I have a large flat white, please? To go, thanks.
Say could I have, not give me. Polite is normal here.
🏨 Hotel check-in
Hi, I have a reservation under the name Timofey. For two nights.
Use under the name, not on the name.
🤝 Meeting introduction
Nice to meet you. I work at Lukoil, in the strategy team.
Use at for company, in for department.
🕓 Ask to leave work
Would it be all right if I left early today? I have a family matter.
Formal but not stiff. Family matter = short and enough.
🚕 In a taxi
Could you drop me at the corner, please? Then I'll walk.
Drop me at — the natural way to say “stop the car and let me out here”.
📧 Short business email
Hi Marco, could you send me the updated file today? Thanks — Timofey.
Two lines is enough. Short is professional here.
Section 07 · Reading A day at the office — spot the tenses
Section 07

Reading · A day at the office

Short business text. Read it once at your pace, then answer four multiple-choice questions. All four Present tenses live inside.

📖 read → tap answer · auto-check

Timofey works at Lukoil in the strategy team. Every morning he checks his email and drinks a large flat white on the way to the office. Today he is preparing a big presentation for a client from Milan — the meeting starts in three hours and he still is finishing the numbers slide.

He has already sent the draft to his boss, but the boss hasn't replied yet. Marco, his colleague, has been working on the same file since Monday and looks tired. “I have been checking these figures for two hours,” Marco says. “Could you help me?”

Timofey nods. Right now they are opening the spreadsheet together. In half an hour the client call begins. Everything is going to be fine — they have handled harder days before.

1. Where does Timofey work?
✅ “Timofey works at Lukoil in the strategy team” — Present Simple, a fact about his job.
2. What is Timofey doing at the moment of the story?
✅ “Today he is preparing a big presentation” — Present Continuous = right now.
3. What has Timofey already done?
✅ “He has already sent the draft to his boss” — Present Perfect = result now.
4. How long has Marco been checking the figures?
✅ “I have been checking these figures for two hours” — Present Perfect Continuous = process, still going, focus on duration.

Listening · Marco calls Timofey

Short business call. Listen twice — first for the story, second for the detail. Open the script only after you tried both times.

🎧 listen → answer · script hidden

📜 Open script

Marco: Hi Timofey, are you busy right now?

Timofey: Yes, I'm working on the client deck. What's up?

Marco: I've been trying to open the numbers file since nine, but it keeps crashing. Have you saved a backup?

Timofey: I've already sent the backup to your email. Check the folder “Milan · draft 3”. I usually drop everything there.

Marco: Perfect. Have you spoken to the client today?

Timofey: Not yet. They haven't confirmed the time. I'm waiting for their reply.

Marco: Got it. I'll open the file now and call you back in ten minutes.

1. What is Timofey doing when Marco calls?
✅ “I'm working on the client deck” — Present Continuous, right now.
2. Since when has Marco been trying to open the file?
✅ “I've been trying to open the numbers file since nine” — Present Perfect Continuous + since.
3. Has Timofey already sent the backup?
✅ “I've already sent the backup to your email” — Present Perfect, result now.
4. Where does Timofey usually keep drafts?
✅ “I usually drop everything there” — Present Simple, routine (“usually”).
