08

Frequency adverbs · where do they go? · 10 sentences

Choose the sentence with the correct position of always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never.

Rule: a frequency adverb goes before a main verb, after the verb be, and between an auxiliary and the main verb.
· I always drink tea. (before main verb)
· She is always late. (after be)
· He has never flown to Rome. (between aux and main)
10 questions · position of the frequency adverb
1. Which sentence is correct?
→ always goes before the main verb drink.
2. Which sentence is correct?
→ After be: She is always on time.
3. Which sentence is correct?
→ never goes before the main verb arrives.
4. Which sentence is correct?
→ usually before the main verb take. (Note: not "the work" — just "work".)
5. Which sentence is correct?
→ After be: My flight is often delayed.
6. Which sentence is correct?
→ Between aux (has) and main verb (been): He has never been.
7. Which sentence is correct?
→ sometimes before the main verb (it can also start the sentence, but never split verb + object).
8. Which sentence is correct?
→ Between aux is and calling: She is rarely calling.
9. Which sentence is correct?
→ Before the main verb (again: "for work", not "for the work").
10. Which sentence is correct?
→ After be: The hotel is always full…
