definite

adjective · /ˈdefɪnət/ · ру́сское: определённый, точный, уверенный
1️⃣ Clear, certain, no doubt — когда что-то точно решено или ясно.
"Is it a definite yes?" — "Yes, absolutely."
"We don't have a definite plan yet, but we're thinking about Sochi."
2️⃣ Grammar term — «the definite article» = определённый артикль «the» (vs. indefinite article «a / an»).
"Use the definite article when you mean a specific thing: the book on the table."
3️⃣ Adverb form — definitely = точно, определённо.
"I'm definitely coming to the party!"
"That was definitely the best pizza in Moscow."
