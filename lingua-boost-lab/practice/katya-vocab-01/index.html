definite

adjective · /ˈdefɪnət/ · определённый, точный, уверенный
1️⃣ Clear, certain — точно решено.
"Is it a definite yes?" · "We don't have a definite plan yet."
2️⃣ the definite article = определённый артикль «the» (vs indefinite «a/an»).
"Use the definite article when you mean a specific thing."
3️⃣ definitely = точно, определённо.
"I'm definitely coming!" · "That was definitely the best pizza."
