Verb to be — am / is / are

Not started

Без am/is/are — никак. «Я устал», «это вкусно», «они дома» — везде нужен глагол-связка.

Reminder: I → am · he / she / it → is · you / we / they → are. Nothing else.
Ex.1 · Choose am / is / are · 10 items
  1. I tired today.
  2. She at home right now.
  3. They students at my school.
  4. It cold in the morning.
  5. We friends since 2020.
  6. He a doctor.
  7. You my best friend.
  8. My parents in Moscow.
  9. Our new flat very quiet.
  10. The kids at school now.
Ex.2 · Questions · 5 MCQ
1. ___ you tired?
"you" всегда идёт с "are".
2. ___ he at home?
"he/she/it" → is.
3. ___ I right?
"I" → am.
4. They ___ not from Moscow.
"they" → are, отрицание — aren't.
5. — Are you a teacher? — Yes, I ___.
Короткий ответ повторяет вспомогательный: Yes, I am.

Simple vs Continuous — the choice

Not started

now → Continuous. Every day → Simple. Stative verbs (like, know, want) → всегда Simple.

Ex.1 · Pick the correct tense · 8 MCQ
1. Right now, I ___ a hoodie.
"right now" → Continuous · am wearing.
2. I ___ coffee every morning.
"every morning" → Simple · drink.
3. Sorry, I ___ what you mean.
understand — stative, только Simple.
4. Look! The kids ___ in the park.
"Look!" — этот момент → Continuous.
5. She ___ her sister very much.
love — stative, только Simple + -s (she).
6. The train ___ at 7 every day.
Расписание → Simple · leaves.
7. This week I ___ from home.
"this week" — временно → Continuous.
8. I ___ tea. Only coffee.
like — stative.

Not started

Short story built from all the year's vocab. Read once, then answer True / False / Not Stated.

My first week in Vancouver

Hi! My name is Denis. I am 12 years old and I usually live in Moscow with my family. Right now I am spending one week in Vancouver — my dad is here on business and he takes me with him every summer.

Our flat is in a very lively neighborhood in downtown. There is a small park across the road and there are two coffee shops on the corner. My landlord is a friendly man from Toronto. He says the area is safe at night and very convenient — the subway is nearby and the drugstore is only two blocks away.

Every morning I have breakfast at eight, and then I go for a walk with my dad. Today we are going to visit Stanley Park — my dad promised. I love this park because it is green and quiet, and there are old trees by the water. Later we will take the SkyTrain to Chinatown to eat noodles. My dad says the number 3 bus stops right by the Millennium Gate.

It is chilly this morning, so I am wearing a hoodie and sneakers. Vancouver weather is different from Moscow — it often rains, but it is never very cold. I like it here, but I still miss my cat. She is waiting for me at home.

Ex.1 · True / False / Not Stated · 10 items
1. Denis lives in Vancouver.
He usually lives in Moscow — he is only spending one week in Vancouver.
2. Denis is 12 years old.
"I am 12 years old."
3. The neighborhood is quiet.
The neighborhood is "very lively" — the opposite of quiet.
4. The landlord is from Toronto.
"My landlord is a friendly man from Toronto."
5. The drugstore is opposite the park.
The text says the drugstore is "two blocks away" but doesn't say where the park is relative to it.
6. Denis has breakfast at 8 a.m.
"Every morning I have breakfast at eight."
7. They are going to visit Stanley Park today.
"Today we are going to visit Stanley Park" — a plan.
8. Number 5 bus goes to Chinatown.
The text says the number 3 bus stops by the Millennium Gate.
9. Vancouver is often very cold.
"It often rains, but it is never very cold."
10. Denis has a dog at home in Moscow.
He mentions a cat waiting for him, but nothing about a dog.

Ex.1 · Rewrite the sentence · fix the mistake · 10 items
Твои 5 привычных косяков: DO vs ARE · выпадение -s после does · Simple вместо Continuous · Are you + verb вместо Do you + verb · пропуск артикля the. Читай, найди ошибку, напиши правильно.

  2. ❌ Are you like tea? →
  3. ❌ Does she wears a coat today? →
  4. ❌ I wear now a hoodie. →
  5. ❌ Let's go to market. → Let's go to
  6. ❌ Does he lives in Moscow? →
  7. ❌ Are you have a car? →
  8. ❌ Right now she cook dinner. → Right now she
  9. ❌ I go to park on Sundays. → I go to
  10. ❌ Do he like coffee? →
Быстрое правило: обычные действия · привычки → Do / Does + глагол без -s. Прямо сейчас · Look! / Listen! → am / is / are + V-ing. Never Are you like/go/have — это Do-территория.
