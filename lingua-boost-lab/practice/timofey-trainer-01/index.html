02

Mix of tenses · 8 предложений

Выбери правильную форму. После клика — объяснение.

Present Simple · Continuous · Perfect · Past Simple
1. I usually ___ to work by car, but today I'm walking.
→ "usually" = регулярное действие → Present Simple (go).
2. She ___ a presentation right now — don't disturb her.
→ "right now" = действие в момент речи → Present Continuous.
3. Last quarter our sales ___ by 15%.
→ "last quarter" = законченный период в прошлом → Past Simple.
4. I ___ already ___ the schedule for next week.
→ "already" + результат сейчас → Present Perfect.
5. My tutor ___ me yesterday that I should focus on speaking.
→ TELL someone (told me), но SAY (to someone) — без прямого me.
6. Banking ___ a strict regulatory environment.
→ Общее правило / факт → Present Simple.
7. When I was a student, I ___ as a tutor on weekends.
→ "When I was a student" = период в прошлом → Past Simple.
8. Look! He ___ into oncoming traffic again!
→ "Look!" = действие происходит сейчас → Present Continuous.

04

-ing forms · 3 функции

Перетащи каждое -ing слово в правильную корзину: gerund (= noun), participle (= adj / часть perfect), continuous (= в составе времени).

Правило 3 в 1: одно окончание -ing может быть герундием (как существительное: Hiking is fun), причастием (как прилагательное: a charming café) или частью времени Continuous (I am preparing). Открой схему в фото-блоке если забыл.
Hiking is fun. a charming café I am preparing reports. Banking pays well. a tiring meeting She is checking the schedule. Labelling is important. an exciting opportunity They are crediting our account. Crediting requires trust. a booming sector We are sharing the costs.

05

said vs told · 6 предложений

TELL someone что-то · SAY (to someone) что-то. После урока «said me» — это ошибка. Запомни: said me ❌ → told me ✓.

1. My tutor ___ me to focus on speaking.
→ told me (с прямым me — это TELL).
2. She ___ that crediting policies will tighten.
→ said that... (без прямого me — это SAY).
3. Can you ___ me the schedule, please?
→ tell me.
4. He ___ to me he wanted a higher band score.
→ said to me (с предлогом TO — это SAY).
5. Could you ___ me about your last job?
→ tell me.
6. Anna ___ that the response was intelligible.
→ said that...

Mini-write · 5–7 предложений

Напиши абзац о своей будущей работе. Используй минимум 5 слов из конспекта (любых).

Target: 5–7 sentences · 70–120 words · 5+ new wordswords: 0
🗝 Sample model (открыть после своей попытки)

I work in the economic sphere — specifically in corporate economics and enterprise logistics. My team handles crediting decisions and the company's overall finance strategy. Every Monday my schedule starts with a sales review, and on Fridays we share the results with management. Honestly, the pace is fast, but I enjoy it. After work I sometimes go hiking with friends — it helps me think more clearly. Next year I want to study management abroad — that is why I am working with a tutor on my English.
