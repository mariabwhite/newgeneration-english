📖 Part B · Now read the text · 6 True / False / Not Stated

🌅 Dawn at the Observatory

At six in the morning Anya and grandma walk up the hill. The sea is pink. The air is cold. «Today you will see the future,» grandma says.

A wooden observatory stands on the cliff. The door creaks. Five brass telescopes shine in the first sunlight.

«Look through Telescope One. Tell me what you will do today.» Anya looks. «I will call Masha. I will drink hot tea. I will finish my book.»

Telescope Two is longer. «What are you going to study at university?» Anya sees her future self with a camera. «I am going to be a nature photographer.»

Telescope Three is small. «What are you doing tomorrow?» «I am meeting my cousin at four. We are flying to Bergen on Friday.»

Telescope Four points at the harbour. «The ferry leaves at seven fifteen every morning,» grandma reads from the schedule. «The sun rises at six forty-two tomorrow.»

Telescope Five is golden. «By 2040 you will have travelled to twenty countries,» grandma whispers. «By the time you are thirty you will have learned three languages.»

The sun climbs. The horizon turns orange. «Future is a direction,» grandma says, «not a mystery. When you come here next year, I will give you the keys.»

6. Anya and grandma arrive at the observatory in the evening.
7. The observatory is made of stone.
8. Anya promises to call her friend Masha today.
9. Anya wants to be a doctor in the future.
10. Anya is flying to Bergen next Friday.
11. The ferry costs twenty euros.

🧭 Theory · 5 future forms side-by-side

1 карта · все формулы

2 · смотри на пять телескопов сразу · запомни формулу + сигнал каждого

🔭 1 · Future Simple (will)

I will / won't + V

Решение прямо сейчас · обещание · предсказание · просьба / предложение (Shall I...?)

I will call you tonight. It will rain tomorrow. Shall I open the window?

signals: tomorrow · next week · in 2030 · probably · I think · maybe

🌅 2 · Be going to

I am/is/are going to + V

План, принятый заранее · видимое доказательство (I see → it's going to rain)

I am going to study law. Look at the clouds — it is going to rain.

signals: I've decided · I plan · look (evidence) · obviously

📅 3 · Present Continuous · for future

I am/is/are + V-ing

Договорённость с конкретным временем / местом / человеком (уже всё решено)

I am meeting Tom at 5. We are flying to Bergen on Friday.

signals: at 5 · on Friday · tonight · tomorrow + person/place

🚆 4 · Present Simple · for timetable

V / V-s

Расписание (поезд / самолёт / кино / урок) — не ты решаешь, а оно само едет

The train leaves at 7:15. The film starts at 8.

signals: timetables, schedules, programmes · at 7 · at 20:00

🏆 5 · Future Perfect

I will have + V3

Будет закончено ДО какого-то момента / времени в будущем

By 2030 I will have finished school. By 8 PM they will have arrived.

signals: by + time · by the time · by then · before Friday

⏳ 6 · Time clause rule

when / if / as soon as / until / before + Pres Simple

После этих слов НЕ ставим will — ставим Present Simple!

When you come — I will give you the keys. If it rains, we'll stay inside.

words: when · if · as soon as · until · before · after · the moment

🔭 Telescope 1 · Future Simple (will)

10 drill · promises · predictions · offers

🔭

Telescope 1 · will / won't · Shall I...?

will + V

I will go · She won't come · Will they help? · Shall I open the door?

use for: spontaneous decision · promise · prediction · request · offer (Shall I...?)

Telescope 2 · am/is/are going to + V

going to + V

I'm going to study · She isn't going to come · Are they going to leave?

📅 Telescope 3 · Present Continuous for arrangements

8 drill · time + place + person

5 · третий телескоп маленький и точный · показывает уже назначенные встречи

📅

Telescope 3 · am/is/are + V-ing (future meaning)

V-ing + time

I'm meeting Tom at 5 · We're flying to Bergen on Friday · Jane isn't coming tonight

use when: there's already a time + place + person. Easy test: can you write it in your calendar? → yes = Present Continuous.

⚠️ NOT for predictions (Tomorrow it is raining → it will / is going to rain)

Drill · 8 вопросов (все с time/place markers)

1. I ___ (see) the dentist at 3 o'clock tomorrow.
2. Anya ___ (meet) her cousin at 4 p.m.
3. We ___ (fly) to Bergen on Friday morning.
4. ___ you ___ (do) anything on Saturday evening?
5. Grandma ___ (stay) with us for the whole weekend.
6. Sorry, I can't come — I ___ (babysit) my little sister tonight.
7. My parents ___ (not / come) to the show on Sunday.
8. What time ___ you ___ (leave) the house on Monday?

🚆 Telescope 4 · Present Simple for timetables

6 drill · trains · films · schedules

6 · четвёртый телескоп смотрит на расписания · не ты решаешь — оно само едет

🚆

Telescope 4 · V / V-s (future meaning for schedules only)

V-s + schedule

The train leaves at 7:15 · The film starts at 8 · Our lesson begins at 11

use only for: timetables, schedules, programmes, cinema, trains, planes, buses, lessons.

⚠️ NOT for personal plans (I leave at 7:15 → I am leaving at 7:15)

Drill · 6 вопросов (timetable only)

1. The 7:15 train ___ in Bergen at 11:40.
2. The film ___ at 8 o'clock tonight.
3. The sun ___ at 6:42 tomorrow.
4. What time ___ the museum ___ on Sundays?
5. Our lesson ___ at 11:00 and ___ at 12:30.
6. The flight to Oslo ___ at 10:05 every morning.

🏆 Telescope 5 · Future Perfect

8 drill · «by + time»

7 · самый золотой телескоп — показывает что УЖЕ будет сделано ДО какого-то момента

🏆

Telescope 5 · will have + V3

will have + V3

By 2030 I will have finished school · By 8 PM they will have arrived · By the time you call, I will have left

signals: by + time · by then · by the time · before Friday · in 2 years' time

V3 = 3rd form (travelled / learned / written / done / seen). Same as Present Perfect, but after will have.

Drill · 8 вопросов (Afanasyeva ex. 19/22)

1. By 2040 Anya ___ (travel) to twenty countries.
2. By the end of the term we ___ (finish) three novels.
3. By 10 o'clock the shop ___ (close).
4. By the time grandma arrives, Anya ___ (bake) the cake.
5. By next Monday the postman ___ (bring) the letter.
6. By the age of 30, I ___ (learn) three languages.
7. The sun ___ (not / rise) by 5 a.m. in December.
8. By 2027 scientists ___ (not / find) a cure for every disease.

⏳ Rule · Time clauses · after when / if / as soon as / until

8 drill · no «will» after these words

8 · главное правило года · после when/if/as soon as/until пишем Present Simple, НЕ will

⏳

Rule · After when / if / as soon as / until / before / after → Present Simple

Pres Simple + will

❌ When he will come, I will tell him · ✅ When he comes, I will tell him

❌ If it will rain, we will stay · ✅ If it rains, we will stay

words: when · if · as soon as · until · before · after · the moment · unless

⚠️ В main clause (главной части) — will остаётся. Только в придаточной с when/if — Present Simple!

Drill · 8 вопросов (Afanasyeva ex. 13/31)

1. When the lesson ___ (finish), we ___ (go) home.
2. If it ___ (not / rain), we ___ (play) football.
3. As soon as I ___ (see) him, I ___ (tell) him the news.
4. I ___ (wait) here until you ___ (come) back.
5. We ___ (have) dinner before grandma ___ (arrive).
6. If you ___ (not / help) me, I ___ (not / finish) this task.
7. The moment Anya ___ (open) the door, grandma ___ (hug) her.
8. I ___ (phone) you after I ___ (get) home.
