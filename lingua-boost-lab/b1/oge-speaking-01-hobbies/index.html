Reading · Tasks 13-19 · True / False / Not stated

Read the text below and decide whether each statement is True, False or Not stated according to the information in the text.

13-19Reading · T/F/NS · 7 items

School years in Russia today

In modern Russia compulsory education officially lasts nine years, from the first to the ninth grade. Almost all children start school at the age of seven, although some parents send their children a year earlier. During the first four years pupils have one main teacher who teaches most subjects, and the timetable usually has four or five lessons a day.

Middle school begins in the fifth grade. At this stage students get a separate teacher for every subject, and the number of lessons goes up to six or seven per day. English becomes a serious subject: most schools have three lessons a week, and gymnasiums often have five. Alongside the basic timetable, ninth-graders start preparing for the OGE, the state examination they take at the end of the year.

After the ninth grade students have two options. They can stay at school for two more years — the tenth and the eleventh grade — and then take the EGE and enter a university. Or they can go to a professional college, where they immediately begin to learn a real profession, such as programmer, cook, medical assistant or engineer. Around forty per cent of ninth-graders choose the second option every year, and this number is growing.

S1Speaking · read aloud · 2 pts

The text · How teenagers today choose their future job

The world of work is changing very quickly, and modern teenagers face a difficult question much earlier than their parents did. According to a recent survey, more than sixty per cent of Russian ninth-graders already have at least one profession in mind, and almost half of them have discussed the idea with their family. The most popular choices are still doctor, IT engineer, designer and teacher, but new jobs, such as data analyst or content creator, are becoming more attractive every year.

Career specialists say that a good choice depends on three things: your interests, your school results and the demand on the job market. They also advise young people not to be afraid of changing their mind. In fact, most adults today have already worked in two or three different fields, and this experience often helps them find the profession that truly makes them happy.

S2

Speaking · Task 2 · Answer 6 questions · 6 points

Answer 6 questions from the electronic assistant. You have 40 seconds per question. Use full sentences, at least 2-3 phrases each.

S2Speaking · dialogue · 6 pts
Golden rule: never answer with a single word or with "yes" / "no". Always give a full sentence and extend it with because / for example / such as.
Q1What school subjects are you most interested in and why?
I am most interested in English, biology and computer science, because they are connected with what I want to do in the future. I also enjoy history when the teacher tells us real stories, but I am not really into physics. Overall, I prefer subjects where we discuss things and not only solve formulas.
Q2Do Russian pupils have enough time for extra classes after school?
Honestly, most Russian pupils in the ninth grade do not have a lot of free time, because they have to prepare for the OGE. However, many of us still find one or two evenings a week for extra English or maths. It is hard, but these classes really help when the exams get closer.
Q3How can school help you prepare for your future career?
School can help in several ways. First of all, it gives us a wide knowledge base, so we can decide which subjects we really enjoy. Besides, our teachers organise meetings with specialists from different fields, and we sometimes visit companies on excursion days. All of that helps us understand what a real working day looks like.
Q4What job would you like to do in the future?
In the future I would like to become a graphic designer or an illustrator. I have been drawing since I was seven, and now I create small comics on my tablet almost every day. I know it is not the easiest career, but I really believe that a job you enjoy is worth the effort.
Q5Do many Russian teenagers take part-time jobs? Why or why not?
Not many Russian teenagers take part-time jobs during the school year, because studies and exam preparation take almost all their time. However, quite a lot of my classmates work during the summer holidays — for example, as camp helpers, couriers or tutors for younger children. It is a good way to earn some pocket money and learn responsibility.
Q6What advice would you give to a friend who cannot choose a profession?
I would give my friend two pieces of advice. First, try to list the activities that make you feel happy and full of energy — often your future job is hidden there. Second, do not be afraid to change your mind later, because most adults today work in a completely different field from the one they dreamed of at fifteen.

⏱ 1.5 min prep🎙 ≤ 2 min answer📏 10-12 sentences
▶ Model monologue A · ~ 1 min 40 sec

IntroNow I am going to give a talk about choosing a career.

Bullet 1To begin with, people usually choose a career for two main reasons: because they truly love the job, or because it is well-paid. Moreover, some young people simply follow in their parents' footsteps, especially in families of doctors or teachers.

Bullet 2Speaking of the age, most teenagers in Russia begin to think seriously about their future profession at fourteen or fifteen, when they have to choose subjects for the OGE. However, small children often say what they want to be already at kindergarten, though these dreams usually change many times.

Bullet 3There are several good ways to prepare for a future profession. First of all, you should read about it and, if possible, meet real specialists or try a short internship. Learning English also helps a lot, because most modern jobs require at least basic knowledge of the language.

Bullet 4As for me, I would like to become a graphic designer, because I have been keen on drawing since I was seven. In my opinion, it is very important to choose a career that you truly enjoy, since you will spend at least eight hours a day at work.

OutroThat is all I wanted to tell you. Thank you for listening.
