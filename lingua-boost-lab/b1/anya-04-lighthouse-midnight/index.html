📖 PART B · Read the story (теперь глазами по тексту)

The Lighthouse at Midnight

Anya lives in a small village by the sea. Every autumn, when the wind gets cold, people tell the same story: the old lighthouse on the cliff is empty — but at midnight, a warm light appears in every window.

Tonight is 31 October. Anya is standing at the bottom of the lighthouse. The wind is blowing, waves are hitting the rocks, and green seaweed is dancing around her boots. A friendly ghost cat is sitting on the step, licking its paw.

«Last year,» Anya said to the cat, «my grandmother climbed this lighthouse. She saw a wonderful light at the top. She came home with a secret book — and never opened it.»

While Anya was speaking, the cat was listening. The moon was rising. Suddenly a bell was ringing somewhere inside — not loud, but clear.

«I will climb,» Anya whispered. «I will reach the top. I won't be scared. Tomorrow I will tell grandma what I saw.»

The cat meowed. «I am going to help you,» it seemed to say. «We are going to climb all six floors — together. And on the top, we are going to find the real secret of the lighthouse.»

Anya takes a deep breath. The door opens with a soft creak. The quest begins. 🕯

🎨 Легенда цветов — 6 времён в тексте

  • голубой = Present Simple (факты / привычки)
  • зелёный = Present Continuous (прямо сейчас)
  • оранжевый = Past Simple (законченное действие)
  • розовый = Past Continuous (процесс в прошлом)
  • салатовый = Future will (решения / обещания)
  • жёлтый = going to (план)

📖 Reading 2 · Grandma's letter

150 слов · Present Perfect + Past

📖 внутри книги бабушки · письмо для внучки · T/F + comprehension

A letter from Grandma

«Dear Anya,

I have written this letter many times in my head — but tonight I finally sit down and put the pen on paper. If you are reading this, you have climbed the lighthouse. You have met the ghost cat. You are brave.

When I was ten, I climbed this lighthouse too. I was scared. The wind was blowing and the cat was sitting on the step — the same cat. He has been living here for a hundred years, maybe more. He is a friend.

On the top floor you will find a small golden box. Inside is my favourite thing: a map of the places I have visited. I have been to Istanbul, Marrakech, Jaipur, Rome, and Lisbon. I bought a souvenir at every market, I wrote a postcard from every temple, I kept a voice memo from every bustling street.

Tomorrow, read the map. Choose one place. Next summer we will go there together. I promise.

Love,
Grandma»

True / False

1. Grandma wrote this letter many times before.
2. The same cat was there when grandma was ten.
3. Grandma has visited 5 cities on her travels.
4. The map is in a wooden box.
5. Grandma promises Anya a trip together.

Comprehension

6. How old was grandma when she first climbed?
7. What did grandma bring from each market?
8. What's inside the small golden box?
