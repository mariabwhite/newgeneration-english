Present Simple · everyday facts & habits

📘 MCQ · 6
First door on the Present floor — small and warm. Anna steps in. The room is full of ticking sounds. Every clock repeats the same second, over and over. This is where always, every day, usually live.

🌸 When to use

Facts, general truths, habits and routines. Repeated actions.

every dayusuallyon Mondaysalwaystwice a week

Form: I / you / we / they + V · he / she / it + V-s

She lives in Moscow and walks to school every morning.
2

Present Continuous · action right now / temporary

✏️ Type-in · 6
Second door. The floor here vibrates — everything is happening now. Anna sees people moving, painting, running mid-step.

🌸 When to use

Action happening at this moment · temporary situation · developing trend · fixed future arrangement.

nowright nowat the momentthis weekLook!

Form: am / is / are + V-ing

Look — it is raining. She is preparing for exams this month.

Type the verb in Present Continuous.

Past Simple · one finished moment

🧩 Word order · 6
Anna walks down to the yellow floor. First past door. The clock here already stopped — every action inside is done, sealed, finished.

🟡 When to use

One finished action at a specific moment in the past.

yesterdaylast weektwo days agoin 2020when I was 10

Form: regular: V + -ed · irregular: V2 (went, saw, wrote)

Anna walked into the tower. She saw twelve clocks.

Tap the words in the right order.

Past Continuous · action in progress in the past

🎴 Match · 8 while / when
Second yellow door. Two clocks tick side by side: one shows a moment (Past Simple), the other shows a long line (Past Continuous). They meet on the wall.

🟡 When to use

Action in progress at a moment in the past · long background action interrupted by a short one · two long parallel actions.

at 5 pm yesterdaywhilewhenall evening

Form: was / were + V-ing

While she was reading, the phone rang. At 8 pm we were having dinner.

Future · will — predictions, promises, decisions now

🎯 Sort by function · 9
Coral floor. First future door. On the ceiling — stars that have not been born yet. Every prediction is a wish.

🪸 When to use

Prediction (I think · probably) · promise · offer · instant decision (made right now).

tomorrownext weekI thinkprobablyI promise

Form: will + V (base) · negative won't

I think it will rain. I will help you — I promise. — Someone's at the door! — I 'll open it.
