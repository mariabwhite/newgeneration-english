Speaking Force · 20 provocation drills

Designed so a plain «yes» or «no» won't do. Every block pushes you to speak: correct a false statement, add because, ask the questions yourself, choose without «depends». All the tenses you've studied (to be · Present Simple · Present Continuous · have got · can · will · going to) and all your topics (routine · home · city · clothes · travel · café · shopping) come alive here.

▸ 20 blocks · speaking drills ▸ ~55 min ▸ correct · answer · ask · choose · describe · monologue
🚫 No plain «yes / no» ✅ Because obligatory 🎤 Min 2 sentences per card ⏱ No pauses longer than 3 sec

«Correct me!» · a false statement — you fix it

reaction drill · 10 lies · all tenses
RuleYou hear a false statement about yourself — correct it with a full sentence in the right tense. No bare «no». Only: «No, I don't! I …» + one detail.
1Denis works at night. → Present Simple
2Denis is wearing sandals right now. → Present Continuous
3Denis has got five cats and two dogs. → have got
4Denis can drive a plane. → can/can't
5Denis is going to visit Australia tomorrow. → going to
6There are three fridges in Denis's kitchen. → There is/are
7It is very hot in Moscow today. → to be (state)
8Denis always drinks cappuccino at midnight. → frequency + PS
9Denis will marry a Hollywood actress next Friday. → will
10Denis's parents live in Antarctica. → apostrophe 's + PS

«My flat · room by room» · 10 forced structures

There is / There are · It is · walk through your flat
RuleWalk (mentally) through your flat room by room — say 10 sentences alternating There is a… · There are… · It is …. Use different rooms (hall → kitchen → living room → bedroom → bathroom).
🚪Hall / corridor: There is a … → mirror / wardrobe / shoe rack / lamp
🍳Kitchen: There are two … → chairs / cups / plates / windows
🍳Kitchen: There is a … → fridge / oven / microwave / sink / kettle
🛋Living room: There is a … → sofa / armchair / TV / lamp / shelf / carpet
🛋Living room: There are … on the shelf. → books / photos / plants / pictures
🛏Bedroom: There is a … → bed / wardrobe / bedside lamp / pillow / blanket
🛁Bathroom: There is a … → mirror / shower / bath / sink / towel
💡Whole flat: It is … → warm / quiet / modern / small / comfortable
🏠Whole flat: It is a … flat. → small studio / one-bedroom / cosy city / modern
🎯Favourite spot: My favourite place is the … because … → kitchen / balcony / armchair
Bonus roundNow say 3 sentences about what is NOT there: «There isn't a … / There aren't any …» — a dishwasher · a balcony · plants · a piano · a second bathroom.

Frequency ladder · specific questions × real answers

7 frequencies · one concrete question each · say YOUR real answer
RuleEach rung has: a real question + a sample answer + words to swap. First read the sample. Then say your own version, replacing the underlined word. Position rule: frequency word goes BEFORE the main verb. After to be — after (bonus box below).
100%always
What do you always do first thing in the morning?
I always drink a cup of coffee before I open my laptop.
swap: coffee → tea · water · orange juice
~90%usually
Where do you usually have lunch on a working day?
I usually have lunch at a small café near the office.
swap: at home · in a restaurant · at my desk
~70%often
How often do you meet friends after work?
I often meet friends on Fridays at our favourite café.
swap: on Fridays → on Saturdays · after 8 p.m. · on weekends
~40%sometimes
Do you go to the gym on Sundays?
I sometimes go to the gym on Sunday morning, if I'm not too tired.
swap: on Sunday morning → in the evening · after work · at lunchtime
~15%rarely
Do you cook dinner at home during the week?
I rarely cook during the week — maybe once or twice a month.
swap: once or twice a month → only on holidays · when guests come
~5%seldom
Do you watch TV in the evening?
I seldom watch TV — I prefer to read a book or listen to music.
swap: read a book → scroll the news · walk with my dog · call my parents
0%never
Do you drink coffee after 6 p.m.?
I never drink coffee after 6 p.m. — it keeps me awake all night.
swap: coffee → strong tea · energy drinks · Coca-Cola
Bonus · to be + adverb (position AFTER be)
 • Are you tired on Friday evenings? → I am always tired on Friday evenings after a long week.
 • Is your wife busy in the morning? → She is often busy — she leaves for work at seven.
 • Are your colleagues late for meetings? → They are rarely late — everyone comes on time.

Wh-questions · pick a word · unfold questions

7 wh-words · click a tile to open 2 questions + sample answers
How it worksTap any wh-word tile below — it unfolds 2 real questions with sample answers. Say YOUR answer first, then compare with the sample. Full sentences only.

💬 What · asks about the thing / action

What do you usually do after work?
I usually go for a short walk in the park before dinner.
What are you wearing right now?
I'm wearing a warm hoodie because it's cold in my office.

📍 Where · asks about the place

Where does your best friend live?
He lives in a small town about two hours from Moscow.
Where do you have lunch during the week?
I have lunch at a small café near the office, usually alone with a book.

⏰ When · asks about the moment / period

When do you finish work on Fridays?
I finish around six p.m., a bit earlier than other days.
When are you going on your next holiday?
I'm going in October, right after my big project ends.

🤔 Why · asks for the reason

Why do you prefer this neighbourhood?
Because there's a park nearby and the streets are quiet in the evening.
Why are you learning English?
Because I want to feel confident when I travel abroad.

🔁 How often · asks about frequency

How often do you go jogging in the park?
I go jogging twice a week, usually on Tuesdays and Sundays.
How often does your family visit you?
They visit me once a month, usually on the second Saturday.

💰 How much / How many · asks about quantity

How much does a taxi cost from your home to the airport?
A taxi costs around fifteen hundred rubles, if there's no traffic.
How many rooms are there in your flat?
There are two rooms, plus a small kitchen and a bathroom.

🕒 What time · asks about the exact clock time

What time do you usually get up on a working day?
I usually get up at seven a.m., even on weekends.
What time does the nearest café open on Sundays?
I think it opens at nine a.m., but I'm not completely sure.
