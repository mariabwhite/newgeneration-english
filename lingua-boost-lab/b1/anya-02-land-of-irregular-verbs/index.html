1

Was / Were · Past of «to be»

📘 Full teach · 8 MCQ
Before the dream begins — the smallest, most important verb of them all. Past of «to be».

⚡ The rule in one glance

PresentPastWho
am / iswasI · he · she · it · one thing
arewereyou · we · they · many things
Julia was at home yesterday. The lights were bright.

Negative: was not = wasn't · were not = weren't — «She wasn't at school. The seats weren't dirty.»

Question: Was + subj? · Were + subj? — «Was she at the dentist?» «Were you tired?»

Split-second rule: singular → was, plural → were.

1Yesterday Julia ___ at home.
→ Julia = one person → was.
2The seats ___ comfortable.
→ seats = plural → were.
3It ___ gloomy outside an hour ago.
→ it = singular → was.
4The lights ___ very bright.
→ lights = plural → were.
5The girl ___ bored at school yesterday. (negative)
→ girl = singular → wasn't.
6The questions ___ difficult. (negative)
→ questions = plural → weren't.
7«___ your friend at the party?» — question
→ your friend = singular → Was.
8«___ you tired last night?» — question
→ «you» → always were.
2

Past Simple regular · V + -ed

📘 Full teach + -ed sounds
One clear finished moment in the past. Yesterday, last night, two days ago, an hour ago.

⚡ Formula

subject + V-ed · «Julia walked home.» «They opened the door.»

Negative: subject + didn't + V (base) · «She didn't walk.» — never «didn't walked».

Question: Did + subject + V (base)? · «Did you open the box?»

Time markers that trigger Past Simple: yesterday · last night / week / year · two days ago · in 2019 · an hour ago.

🔊 Pronunciation of -ed · three sounds

Ending sound-ed sounds likeExamples
voiceless: p k f s ʃ tʃ/t/walked, watched, washed, liked
voiced + vowels: b g v z m n l r/d/played, lived, answered, opened
t or d (adds a syllable)/ɪd/wanted, started, visited, needed

Drill · pick the right form

1Julia ___ home slowly. (walk)
→ Past Simple regular: walk + ed.
2She ___ the dentist yesterday. (not visit)
→ Negative: didn't + base form.
3«___ you ___ the window?» — question about (open)
→ Question: Did + subj + base form.
4How is «visited» pronounced at the end?
→ «visit» ends in /t/ → -ed adds a full syllable /ɪd/.
5How is «watched» pronounced at the end?
→ /tʃ/ is voiceless → -ed = /t/.
6How is «answered» pronounced at the end?
→ /r/ is voiced → -ed = /d/.

The dream begins · Julia falls asleep

🌙 Story · 5 MCQ
Now the story starts. Read the opening, then answer five short questions.
Chapter 1 · The Dream Begins

It was late. Julia closed her book, walked to the window and looked at the dark sky. The lights in the street were quiet, and the whole town was asleep.

She climbed under the blanket and closed her eyes. She was tired. She was almost asleep.

Then, very slowly, the walls of her room started to change. The ceiling opened like a book. And Julia opened her eyes in a place that was not her room at all.

She was standing on a narrow road between crooked houses. The sky above her was purple with tiny stars, and a warm wind touched her hair. Somewhere in the distance a bell rang.

«Where am I?» she whispered. Nobody answered. She started to walk.

Q1What time of day does the story begin?
→ «It was late … the whole town was asleep.»
Q2«Julia opened her eyes and she was still in her room.» — True / False / Not Stated?
→ She opened her eyes in a place that was not her room at all. False.
Q3What colour was the sky above her?
→ «The sky above her was purple with tiny stars.»
Q4«There was a river next to the road.» — True / False / Not Stated?
→ A river is never mentioned. Not Stated.
Q5What did Julia hear in the distance?
→ «Somewhere in the distance a bell rang.»

Chapter 2 · The Signboard

Julia walked a little further and stopped. A wooden sign stood in the middle of the road. On it, in large golden letters, was written:

« IN THIS LAND, ACTIONS DO NOT FOLLOW SIMPLE RULES. »

«Simple rules?» she whispered. «What does that mean?»

Three tiny figures · «We are ONE verb»

🎭 Story + 5 MCQ
Three shouting figures. One argument. And one small idea that changes everything.
Chapter 3 · Three Tiny Figures

Three tiny figures jumped out of the shadow and started arguing loudly.

The first one shouted: «I am STAND!»
The second one crossed his arms and said: «I am STOOD!»
The third one spun in a circle and laughed: «And I am STOOD again!»

«No, I am first!»
«No, I am important!»
«No, without me nothing makes sense!»

Julia smiled. «Who are you three?» she asked.

All three stopped and looked at her.
«We are one verb,» they said together. «Three faces of the same word.»

«Because in this country,» said the first proudly, «every verb has three forms. Base. Past. And sometimes a third one.»
«You cannot enter our land if you do not know us.»

Q1How many tiny figures met Julia?
→ Three tiny figures.
Q2What did the three figures shout?
→ STAND · STOOD · STOOD again.
Q3What did they finally tell Julia about themselves?
→ «We are one verb — three faces of the same word.»
Q4How many forms does every verb have in this land?
→ Base · Past · sometimes a third.
Q5«The figures were wearing yellow hats.» — True / False / Not Stated?
→ Not mentioned. Not Stated.

Reading · rooms of the verbs

📖 Story + 5 T/F/NS
Julia walks deeper into the land. Every house has its own room, full of verb-people. Read carefully, then answer.
Chapter 4 · Rooms of the Verbs

Julia opened the blue door and stepped into the Room of WENT. Small verb-people walked in circles. Some studied a notebook, some answered questions, some clapped quietly as if they were in a theatre.

She crossed the room and opened a green door. Inside sat three very serious verbs at a wooden table. They wore dark glasses and looked very busy: Do wrote in a notebook, Did spoke quietly, and Done checked a big black book. «What will you do now?» they asked together.

The last door opened onto the Room of RUN. Everything was moving. Catch ran after a fast little cat. Jump jumped over a table. Fly flew near the ceiling. Fall fell on the floor but stood up again immediately. Nothing stayed still.

«This is a country of action,» Julia whispered. «Nothing rests. Every verb lives.»

Q1What was the name of the first room Julia entered?
→ Blue door first, Room of WENT.
Q2How many verbs sat at the wooden table?
→ Do, Did, Done — three.
Q3«All the verbs in the Room of RUN were sleeping quietly.» — T / F / NS?
→ Nothing stayed still, everything was moving. False.
Q4«The three serious verbs offered Julia a cup of tea.» — T / F / NS?
→ Not mentioned. Not Stated.
Q5«Fall fell on the floor and immediately stood up again.» — T / F / NS?
→ Word-for-word in the text. True.

⚡ Spelling reminders

  • Regular verbs: base + -ed. If verb ends in -y after a consonant → y → i + ed (study → studied).

Reading ×2 · Chapter II · The Past

📖 200-word passage · 8 questions
The silver book opens to a new chapter. It speaks of the past — where every verb has three faces, and where memory shapes who we are.
Chapter II · The Past

The past tells the story of what has happened. We use past forms to speak about time gone by, and the memories that shape who we are. Every action in this land carries three faces: the beginning, the middle, and the ending.

Julia saw the words glow on the page. She read them slowly, one after another. go — went — gone. see — saw — seen. write — wrote — written. take — took — taken. She knew the base forms from school, but the second and third columns were new to her.

«Read them aloud,» whispered a voice. Julia looked around, but nobody was there. The book itself was speaking.

«Every ending is a new beginning,» the book said. «You cannot leave this land until you can speak the past.» So Julia opened her mouth and began: «I went. I saw. I wrote. I took. I was. I knew. I found. I gave.» With every verb, a small star appeared above her head. When she spoke the last one, all eight stars joined together into one bright constellation, and the crooked houses of the village bowed to her.

«Now you belong here,» the book said. «Or, more truly — now the past belongs to you.»
