1A · «Подать в суд» — шесть способов сказать

Все они переводятся как «подать в суд / засудить / обратиться в суд», но register разный. Выбор зависит от того, с кем ты говоришь и о каком деле речь.

ФразаRegisterКогда используетсяПример
sue (someone) for Xcivil case — за деньги или injunctionMarta sued Golden Crust for trademark infringement.
file a lawsuitформальное начало гражданского делаHer lawyer filed a lawsuit on Monday morning.
take someone to courtугроза или описание пост-фактумIf they don't refund me, I'll take them to court.

Коллокации с «sue»: sue someone for damages (за деньги) · sue someone over a contract (из-за) · sue for breach of contract · sue in federal court.

Pick the right phrase for the scenario × 6

Прочитай сценарий, выбери одну из шести фраз, которая по register и смыслу подходит точнее всего.

1. You're arguing with the dry cleaner who ruined your coat; you want to threaten them face to face. You say: «If you don't pay for it, I'll you.»
Informal / spoken threat → take them to court. The formal variants would sound wrong at the counter.
2. The opening line of a federal complaint, drafted by counsel. «Plaintiff will in the United States District Court for the Northern District of California.»
Very formal register, document-level → commence proceedings.
3. A shareholder press release announcing a lawsuit against the board of directors: «Minority shareholders have decided to the board for breach of fiduciary duty.»
Formal legal register in corporate context → bring an action against.
4. A victim of an assault is at the police station, deciding whether to involve the state. The officer asks: «Do you want to ?»
Criminal context, victim-initiated through police → press charges. Civil verbs don't fit here.
5. Marta describes her first legal step to a friend over coffee — one neutral verb, money damages the goal: «I'm going to Golden Crust for trademark infringement.»
Neutral civil verb for private money claim → sue.
6. A press statement by the bakery's PR team, formal civil register: «Marta's Bakery LLC today against Golden Crust Co. in federal court.»
Neutral «formal civil announcement» register → file a lawsuit. (Both filed a lawsuit and commenced proceedings fit grammatically; the press-release default is the shorter one.)

Threatening letters · how to read and write

Три жанра: cease-and-desist, demand letter, without-prejudice settlement opener. С образцами, ключевыми фразами и 4-MCQ.

1I · Письма коллег с угрозами · как читать и писать

Три стандартных жанра. Распознай их — и половина страха уйдёт.

Cease-and-desist letter · «прекрати или мы подадим»
Hartman & Reyes LLP, 14 October 2026

Dear Mr Vasquez,

We represent Marta's Bakery LLC, the owner of the registered trademark "Artisan Harvest Twist" (US Reg. 7,214,988). It has come to our client's attention that Golden Crust Co. is selling a product under the name "Harvest Twist" using substantially identical trade dress. This use constitutes trademark infringement under the Lanham Act.

We hereby demand that Golden Crust Co. immediately: (1) cease all use of the infringing name and packaging; (2) destroy existing inventory; (3) provide an accounting of sales. Failure to comply by 1 November 2026 will leave our client no option but to commence proceedings in federal court.

This letter is sent without prejudice.

Demand letter · «заплати, или в суд»
To: Golden Crust Co., Legal Department

Our client has suffered documented losses of $82,400 as a direct result of your continued use of the infringing product. We hereby demand payment of this sum within fourteen (14) days of receipt of this letter. Should payment not be received by 5 November 2026, our client is prepared to file suit in the United States District Court for the Northern District of California, seeking damages, injunctive relief, and attorneys' fees.

Response · without-prejudice settlement opener
From opposing counsel, 20 October 2026

Without prejudice. While Golden Crust denies the allegations in your letter of 14 October, our client is open to exploring a commercial resolution rather than engage in protracted litigation. We propose a confidential meeting to discuss terms, including a possible licensing arrangement and withdrawal of the disputed product line. Please let us know your client's availability.

Key phrases to recognise: without prejudice (защитная оговорка — письмо нельзя использовать как признание в суде) · we hereby demand · failure to comply by X · leave our client no option but to commence proceedings · injunctive relief · attorneys' fees.

A fixed sum + a payment deadline is the signature of a demand letter.

Settle it peacefully · or the litigation path

Два списка фраз — мирный путь и воинственный, плюс 10-item PEACE / WAR drill. 95% дел не доходят до присяжных — язык «как уладить миром» практичнее.

1J · Как уладить дело миром

  • settle out of court — договориться без решения суда.
  • reach a settlement — достичь договорённости.
  • confidential settlement agreement — с NDA про сумму.
  • mediation — третий нейтральный помогает договориться, не выносит решения.
  • arbitration — арбитр ВЫНОСИТ решение, обязательное для обеих сторон.
  • licensing arrangement — вместо запрета — платить роялти.
  • walking-away deal — каждый несёт свои расходы, иск отзывается.
  • structured settlement — выплата частями.
  • release of claims — истец отказывается от всех будущих претензий по этому делу.
Фразы на переговорах: «We're open to a commercial resolution» · «Would your client consider a confidential settlement?» · «We can agree on a figure, but we'd need a strict NDA» · «Both sides walk away — no admission of liability».

Why most lawsuits never go to trial

To the general public, a lawsuit tends to conjure images of a dramatic jury verdict delivered in a crowded courtroom. In practice, that image is a rounding error. In the United States federal civil system, roughly ninety-five percent of cases that are filed are resolved before a jury is ever empanelled, and in state courts the figure is comparable. Most disputes end in a negotiated settlement, a motion to dismiss, or an order granting summary judgment. The courtroom scenes that dominate popular culture are the exception, not the rule.

The simplest reason is cost. Civil litigation in the United States is expensive long before trial. Discovery — the pre-trial exchange of documents, depositions and interrogatories — routinely consumes six-figure sums even for mid-sized disputes, and for complex commercial matters the bill can reach the tens of millions. Because both sides bear their own fees under the default American rule, each additional month of litigation reduces the amount either party can walk away with. A settlement that leaves everyone slightly disappointed often leaves everyone materially better off than a trial victory purchased at ruinous cost.

A second factor is risk. Juries are unpredictable. A plaintiff with strong evidence may still lose, and a defendant with a solid defence may still face a runaway damages award. Experienced litigators sometimes describe trial as a bet with poor odds and a long tail of downside. A guaranteed, confidential settlement is attractive precisely because it removes the tail risk, often in exchange for a modest discount from the claim's expected value. Insurers, who ultimately fund many defence payouts, apply the same cold logic and frequently push for settlement well before trial.

A third, less visible factor is strategic. Many lawsuits are not primarily about money. Plaintiffs may file to force a competitor to the negotiating table, to compel disclosure of damaging documents, or simply to signal that misconduct carries a price. Once those strategic objectives have been met — once the deal is struck, the information is in hand, or the market has registered the lesson — a drawn-out trial adds little. In such cases, settlement is not a retreat; it is the point of the exercise.

None of this means that trials are irrelevant. Rare though they are, their shadow shapes every negotiation around them. Both sides calibrate their offers against the trial they would otherwise face, which is why apparently generous settlements sometimes reflect weak cases, and apparently harsh settlements sometimes reflect how badly a party wanted to avoid the stand.

Target Constructions · formal English reference

Шесть целевых блоков, которые нужно уметь выдавать на автомате до любого provocation drill. Прочитай формулу → произнеси вслух оба примера → затем переходи к §15 с полным осознанием, какой рычаг ты дёргаешь.

1. Conditionals · formal inversion

1A · Were + S + to + inf, … would …

Формула: формальная замена для If S V(past), … would …

Were the university to focus only on top grades, many hardworking students would be unfairly excluded.

Were the court to reverse its ruling, the entire industry would need to renegotiate its standard contracts.

1B · Should + S + inf, … (imperative / future)

Формула: формальная замена для If you have …

If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us. → Should you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us.

Should the defendant fail to appear, the court will proceed in absentia.

2. Subjunctive · It is essential/vital/crucial that S (should) do

Формула: It is essential/vital/important/crucial/recommended that + S + (should) + inf — без -s в третьем лице.

It is essential that universities recognize both academic achievement and personal effort.

It is recommended that he (should) attend the seminar.

It is crucial that the plaintiff file the complaint within the statute of limitations.

3. Inversion · формальность и усиление

Порядок слов меняется: наречие + вспомогательный глагол + подлежащее.

3A · Rarely / Seldom + aux + S

Students rarely achieve excellence without proper mentoring. → Rarely do students achieve excellence without proper mentoring.

Rarely do students succeed without comfortable study spaces like coffee hubs.

Seldom has a single ruling reshaped so much corporate practice.

3B · Never + aux + S

I have never seen such a beautiful performance. → Never have I seen such a beautiful performance.

Never before had the firm faced a class action of this scale.

3C · Not only + aux + S … but also …

The research center helps students and professors. → Not only does the research center help students, but it also helps professors.

Not only does a tutoring center help children, but it also benefits students academically.

Not only did the merger breach competition law, but it also damaged consumer trust.

3D · No sooner + aux + S … than …

As soon as he had arrived, the meeting began. → No sooner had he arrived than the meeting began.

No sooner had the settlement been announced than the share price rebounded.

4. Cleft Sentences · выделение части предложения

4A · What + S + V + is/was + выделяемая часть

I need a reliable internet connection. → What I need is a reliable internet connection.

A balanced approach is what modern education needs most. → What modern education needs most is a balanced approach.

What students really need is a place that combines social life and study opportunities.

What the court is really deciding is whether the clause itself is enforceable.

4B · It is/was + выделяемая часть + who/that …

The new software increased our productivity. → It was the new software that increased our productivity.

It is the arbitration clause that quietly removes the right to a jury trial.

5. Linkers · academic style

  • Therefore, — формальный вывод (= so).
  • Consequently, — следствие (= as a result).
  • On the other hand, — противопоставление (= however, но мягче).
  • Whereas — контраст внутри одного предложения (= while).

The claim lacked documentary evidence; therefore, the judge dismissed it.

Senior counsel approved the strategy; consequently, the junior associates stopped pushing back.

Litigation is slow and public; on the other hand, arbitration is faster and confidential.

Mediation seeks an agreement, whereas arbitration ends with a binding decision.

6. «Suggest» constructions

6A · suggest + noun / gerund

Формула: suggest + something / doing something.

I suggest a break.

He suggested going to the cinema.

Counsel suggested filing an amended complaint rather than appealing.

6B · suggest + that-clause · самая важная

Формула: suggest + that + S + (should) + base verb. Третье лицо без –s.

The consultant suggested that we should invest in new technology.

I suggest that you see a doctor.

The board suggested that the CEO step down pending the investigation.

Запрещено: I suggest you to see a doctor. — конструкция suggest + someone + to-infinitive не существует.
6C · suggest + wh-word clause

Формула: suggest + what/where/how/when + to-infinitive / clause.

Can you suggest where to buy good coffee around here?

She suggested how we could improve the process.

The partner suggested when the deposition should take place.

6D · suggest + indirect object

Формула: suggest + something + to + someone.

What did you suggest to the manager?

Counsel suggested a lower settlement figure to opposing counsel.

Как использовать этот блок: проговори каждую формулу вслух × 2, затем в §15 при провокации «rebuild this using inversion / cleft / subjunctive» тебе уже не надо вспоминать правило — достаёшь готовую модель и подставляешь свой контент.

Writing · cease-and-desist letter (80–150 words)

Write Marta's cease-and-desist to Golden Crust. 80–150 words. Use the six-move template below; keep the register firm but professional.

Teaching card · anatomy of a cease-and-desist

A cease-and-desist letter is a pre-litigation demand on firm letterhead. Short, specific, and formal — it names what the recipient must stop doing, and makes clear what happens if they don't. It follows a near-fixed six-move template:

  1. Identify your client. “This firm represents Marta Nowak, owner of Nowak's Bakery, Elm & Fifth.”
  2. State the right you're protecting. “Our client is the proprietor of the registered trademark ‘Nowak's Twist' and of distinctive trade dress in the twist pattern of her signature loaf.”
  3. State the infringement. “Golden Crust Co. is marketing a product under the name ‘Artisan Harvest Twist' with a materially identical twist pattern.”
  4. Demand to stop. “We demand that you immediately cease and desist from manufacturing, marketing and selling the Artisan Harvest Twist…”
  5. Deadline. “Please provide written confirmation of compliance within fourteen (14) days of receipt of this letter.”
  6. Consequences. “Should you fail to do so, our client will initiate legal proceedings seeking injunctive relief and damages, without further notice.”

§18 · Retell + opinion scaffolds (quick ref)

Retell каркас: It all started when … At first … Shortly after … Once discovery was under way … In the end …

Legal-verb скелет (8+ из этих): alleged · breached · filed · served · moved to dismiss · denied · took depositions · settled · reached a confidential settlement · licensing arrangement vs injunction.

Opinion opening: «The real question is not whether … but whether …»

Ruling: «Were I the judge, I would rule in favour of …» · «On the facts, I would find …»

Formal pivot (обязательно): inversion «Not only did Golden Crust …» · cleft «What makes this case clear is …» · subjunctive «It is essential that … cease …» · Were-S-to «Were I to decide tomorrow …»

Правило: ключи — после ответа, не вместо. Если открываешь ключ до того, как попробовал сам — этот drill не считается пройденным, вернись и переделай без подглядывания.
