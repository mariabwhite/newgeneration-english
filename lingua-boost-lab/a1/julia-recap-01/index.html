Recap 01 — Back to School

Everything from the summer program in one lesson: clothes, prepositions of time and place, present simple vs continuous, was / were, past simple, containers, polite phrases.

Level A2 8 sections ≈ 45–55 min
Section 1

Julia's first school day back

Read the story. Then answer True / False / Not stated.

It was Monday, the first of September. Julia woke up at seven o'clock in the morning.

She put on her white shirt, a dark skirt and her new sneakers. Her old sneakers were too small now.

In the kitchen, her mum was making breakfast. Her little brother John was already eating a sandwich and reading a comic.

Julia had a cup of tea and a packet of biscuits. She put a bottle of water and an apple into her bag.

She walked out of the house at eight. The street was quiet. She walked past a small park, across the road, and up the hill to the school.

At the gate she saw her friend Emma. Emma was wearing a blue dress. They walked into the classroom together.

The first lesson was English. The teacher, Mrs Brown, said: «Welcome back, girls! Today we are speaking about your summer.» Julia was a bit nervous, but she was ready.

Section 3

Was / Were + Past Simple

Choose the correct past form for each gap.
Reminder

I / he / she / it → was ·   you / we / they → were.
Regular verbs: walk → walked. Irregular: go → went, see → saw, buy → bought, put → put, have → had.

Section 4

Present Simple vs Continuous

Read the marker word. Choose the right form.
Reminder

Marker words like now / at the moment / look! → Present Continuous (am / is / are + -ing).
Markers like usually / every day / often / sometimes → Present Simple.

1Look! Julia her notebook.
2Julia usually at seven.
3At the moment Mrs Brown on the board.
4Every morning John a comic at breakfast.
5Now Emma a blue dress.
6Julia often to school with Emma.
7Listen! Someone Russian in the corridor.
8Mum pancakes every Sunday.
