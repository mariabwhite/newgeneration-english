My city — today and 50 years from now

01

Базовая формула · с этого начинается весь урок

Перед стартом проговори вслух каждую рамку. Это твой компас на 90 минут.

1 · What exists
There is / There are
There is a park. There are many shops.
2 · The situation
It is
It is cold. It is a big city.
3 · Usually, always
I + V (Present Simple)
I work in the city. I drink coffee every morning.
4 · Right now
I am + V-ing (Continuous)
I am walking now. I am drinking coffee.

Чейн из 4 фраз про любое место:   «There is a market. It is cold. I wear a coat. I am wearing boots now.»   — собери такую цепочку про свой район за 30 секунд. Вслух.

02

Warmup · 8 правильно построенных вопросов

До сих пор ты в основном отвечал. Сегодня учимся спрашивать. Это даёт тебе власть в разговоре — ты ведёшь беседу, а не догоняешь её. Прочти каждую карточку вслух, потом сразу повтори вопрос без подсказки.

Do + you + V?
Do you live in a big city?
Habit / fact. → Do you work near here? Do you drink coffee?
Are + you + V-ing?
Are you walking right now?
What's happening at this moment. → Are you working? Are you waiting?
Is there / Are there?
Is there a café near here?
Does X exist here? → Are there shops in your street? Is there a bank on the corner?
Where do / Where are?
Where do you usually go on Sundays?
Place questions. → Where are you going now? Where do you work?
What is / What are?
What is your favourite place in the city?
Asking about a thing. → What are you doing today? What is near your house?
How often do?
How often do you go to the park?
Frequency. → How often do you take the bus? How often do you eat out?
Can you tell me…?
Can you tell me where the bank is?
Polite ask. → Can you tell me how to get to the metro? Can you show me on the map?
Why do / Why are?
Why do people love this city?
Reason. → Why are you smiling? Why do you live here?

Speaking drill · 90 seconds: прочти 8 вопросов вслух подряд. Затем закрой страницу мысленно и задай 8 вопросов про мой район — мне. Не повторяй формы — используй все 8 разных.

04

Continuous live · 5 видео-кадров

Каждое видео — 3-5 секунд. Смотри и сразу говори ОДНО предложение в Present Continuous вслух. ▶ услышь подсказку · отметь «Done» когда сказал.

01 Two people on the street They are walking. Where? Why? Who?
02 A student with a cup She is sitting. She is holding… She is feeling…
03 A woman is talking She is speaking. To whom? About what? How does she look?
04 Opening an umbrella It is… She is… Why is she doing this?
05 A hand on a door handle Someone is opening… Where? What is behind it?
05

Two city dialogues · asking the way

Не магазин и не кофейня — те будут на следующих уроках. Сегодня — spotting your way around the city. Прочти каждый диалог вслух за обе роли. Потом за одну роль, представляя ответы другой.

🚶 At the corner · asking for the metro

You
Excuse me, can you tell me how to get to the metro?
Local
Sure. Go straight on this street for two blocks.
You
OK, straight for two blocks. And then?
Local
Then turn right at the traffic lights. The metro is next to the bank.
You
Is it far?
Local
No, only five minutes on foot.
You
Great. Thank you very much!
Local
You're welcome. Have a nice day!
Учи структуру: Excuse me → ask → confirm → check distance → thank

🗺️ A tourist asks you · you are local

Tourist
Hi! I'm a bit lost. Where is the museum?
You
The museum? It's not far. Are you on foot?
Tourist
Yes, I'm walking.
You
OK. Go past the park. The museum is on the other side, opposite the bridge.
Tourist
Is it open today?
You
I think so. It usually opens at ten.
Tourist
Perfect. Thanks a lot!
You
No problem. Enjoy your visit!
Drill · 3 passes: первый медленно с подсказками · второй без подсказок · третий — переверни роли, ты идёшь в обратной паре.

07

A walk through your day · 8 anchored steps

Не «расскажи свободно» — это никогда не работает. Дам 8 шагов, заполни каждый одной строкой по-английски, потом прочти всё подряд вслух как один связный рассказ. Текст сохраняется автоматически — придёшь завтра, ничего не пропадёт.

There is / areWhat is near your home?
e.g. Near my home there is a small park and there are two cafés on my street.
It isWhat is your area like today?
e.g. Today it is sunny but a bit windy.
I usually + VWhat do you usually do in the morning?
e.g. I usually get up at 7 and I drink coffee in the kitchen.
I + V on SundaysWhat about Sundays — what's different?
e.g. On Sundays I do not work. I walk in the park and I read.
I am + V-ingWhat are you doing right now (literally, now)?
e.g. Right now I am sitting at my desk and I am answering these questions.
There are + peopleWhat are the people in your city like?
e.g. There are many busy people on the streets in the morning.
My favourite + becauseYour favourite place and why?
e.g. My favourite place is the small bookshop on the corner because it is quiet.
I like / I do not likeOne thing you love + one thing you don't?
e.g. I like the cafés but I do not like the traffic in the city centre.

Then: прочти все 8 предложений подряд вслух — это твой связный 60-секундный монолог про город. Если в одной строке запнулся — следующая, не лечи прошлую.

08

Speaking finale · Round 1 · today

Шесть необычных вопросов. Не «опиши свой город» — конкретные ситуации. Ответь 2-3 предложениями на каждый. ▶ — услышь вопрос. ✓ Done — когда сказал вслух.

1.A friend from another country is visiting you for one day. Where do you take them? (In the morning I take them to … For lunch we go to … In the evening …)
2.Imagine your city had no cars for one week. What changes? (It is quieter. People are walking. The streets are …)
3.What is one thing in your city that always makes you smile? (There is a … I like it because …)
4.Tell me about a place in your city you do not like. Why? (I do not like … because it is … and there are too many …)
5.What does your street look like at 7 a.m. compared to 7 p.m.? (At 7 a.m. there are … At 7 p.m. it is … and people are …)
6.If a tourist had only one photo to take in your city — what should they photograph? Why? (They should take a photo of … because it is …)
09

Future · will vs going to · rule in 60 seconds

Two ways to talk about future. The difference is not in meaning — it is where the idea came from. Read the four frames out loud first, then we drill.

will · decision right now
I will + V
"It is cold." — "OK, I will close the window." (idea was born this second)
will · prediction
It will + V
Robots will work in cafés in 50 years.
going to · existing plan
I am going to + V
I am going to visit my parents on Sunday. (decided earlier)
going to · evidence in front of you
It is going to + V
Look at the sky — it is going to rain. (you see the signs now)

Short rule in one line:

— will = idea born now (decision, promise) OR prediction about the world.
— going to = plan made earlier, OR signs are showing it now.

Native speakers mix these all the time. Start using both — accuracy will follow.

10

Will-drill · 6 decisions and predictions

Read the situation out loud and react with will. No preparation — first thing that comes to mind. ▶ to hear the prompt. ✓ Done when said.

1.The doorbell is ringing. What will you do? (I will open … / I will check who it is …)
2.I say: "I am thirsty." What will you offer me? (I will bring you water … / I will make tea …)
3.What will the weather be like tomorrow? (It will be … I think it will …)
4.What will cities look like in 100 years? Two predictions. (Cities will be … People will …)
5.Promise me one thing for next week. (I will … I promise.)
6.Will people still read paper books in 50 years? Why or why not? (Yes, they will … / No, they will not because …)
11

Going-to-drill · 6 plans you've already made

Tell me what you already decided earlier. Use going to. Full sentences. ▶ to hear the question.

1.What are you going to do tonight? (I am going to … then I am going to …)
2.What are you going to do this weekend? Two specific plans. (On Saturday I am going to … On Sunday …)
3.Are you going to travel anywhere this summer? (Yes, I am going to visit … / No, I am going to stay in …)
4.Look outside. Is it going to rain or be sunny in the next hour? (It is going to … because I can see …)
5.What are you going to learn next month in English? (I am going to learn … and …)
6.What is the biggest thing you are going to do this year? (I am going to … because …)

14

Speaking finale · Round 2 · your future

Six final questions on the future. Same rule — 2-3 sentences each. ▶ hear · ✓ Done when said.

1.Where are you going to live in 10 years? Same city or somewhere new? (I am going to live in … because …)
2.Will robots take over cafés and shops in 30 years? What do you think? (Yes, they will … / No, because …)
3.What is one big plan you have for next year? (I am going to … because …)
4.Will people still drive normal cars in 50 years? (I think people will / will not … because …)
5.Describe the perfect city of the future. What will it have? What will it NOT have? (My perfect city will have … It will not have …)
6.Are you going to learn another language after English? Which one and why? (I am going to learn … because …)
