📖 How it works

Every country has three sister-words: country (the place), nationality (the people, the adjective), and language (what they speak). «I am from France. I am French. I speak French.»

⚡ Two small traps

Some nationalities have a special noun for the person: 🇬🇧 an Englishman, 🇫🇷 a Frenchman, 🇳🇱 a Dutchman, 🇸🇪 a Swede, 🇵🇱 a Pole, 🇹🇷 a Turk, 🇪🇸 a Spaniard. In everyday talk you can just say «I'm British / French / Dutch / Swedish / Polish / Turkish / Spanish».
Two countries speak Portuguese: 🇵🇹 Portugal AND 🇧🇷 Brazil. English is spoken in 🇬🇧, 🇺🇸, 🇦🇺, 🇨🇦 and many others.

Reading · Julia's postcards

A little world tour · 5 comprehension questions
Julia's world tour · 5 postcards

🇮🇹 «Ciao, mum! I'm in Rome. My friend Marco is Italian — of course! He speaks Italian so fast, but he teaches me two words a day. Yesterday we ate pizza in a small café.»

🇯🇵 «Hi from Tokyo! It is very big. My host sister Yuki is Japanese. She speaks Japanese and a little English. She showed me how to write my name in Japanese letters.»

🇧🇷 «Hola from Brasilia! Well — actually it's «olá», not «hola», because in Brazil people speak Portuguese, not Spanish. That surprised me.»

🇬🇷 «Kalimera from Athens! Sophia is Greek. She showed me the letters of the Greek alphabet. Some of them look like maths!»

🇹🇷 «Last stop: Istanbul. My friend Emre is Turkish. We drank tiny cups of tea. The capital of Turkey is Ankara, not Istanbul — I always forget!»

— Love, Julia

7

Speaking · pick a country card

Choose one · describe it aloud in 5-6 sentences
A🇯🇵My country is Japan. Tell me: what is the capital, what language people speak, one food you know, one word in that language.
B🇮🇹My country is Italy. Where is it? What language do people speak? What do you eat there? What is the capital?
C🇧🇷My country is Brazil. Which continent is it on? What language do Brazilians speak? What is the capital? What holiday is famous there?
D🇬🇷My country is Greece. What is the capital? What alphabet do people use? Name one famous Greek word.
E🇷🇺My country is Russia. What is the capital? What language do people speak? Name one Russian food and one Russian city.

🎯 Model sentence · use for every card

«This is [country]. The capital is [city]. People are [nationality]. They speak [language]. In this country you can eat [food]. One word I know is [word].»
