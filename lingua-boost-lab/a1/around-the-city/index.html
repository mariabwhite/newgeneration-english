Around the city · asking for directions

City vocabulary — streets, parks, cafés, bus stops. Learn how to ask the way and give directions back. Reinforce three common traps: Do vs Are, Present Simple vs Present Continuous, and the article the with places.

Уровень

Длительность
~90 минут
Фокус

Навыки
Vocab · Speaking · Listening · Writing

02

Dialogue · a tourist asks for directions

Read the dialogue silently first, then aloud for both roles. Notice how the local explains the way — short sentences, simple word order.

Tourist
Excuse me, where is the post office?
Извините, где почта?
Local
Go straight on this street.
Идите прямо по этой улице.
Local
Then turn left at the traffic lights.
Затем поверните налево на светофоре.
Local
The post office is on the corner, opposite the bank.
Почта на углу, напротив банка.
Tourist
Is it far?
Это далеко?
Local
No, only five minutes on foot.
Нет, всего пять минут пешком.
Tourist
Thank you very much!
Большое спасибо!
03

Drill · Do or Are?

The most common trap. Do — for action verbs (live, go, like, work). Are — for adjectives, professions, location, long -ing forms.

1. ___ you live in the city?
→ live — обычное действие → Do you live
2. ___ you walking now?
→ walking (-ing) → Are you walking
3. ___ you like coffee?
→ like — обычное действие → Do you like
4. ___ they at home?
→ at home — где они → Are they at home
5. ___ she work in the centre?
→ work — действие (she = 3 лицо → Does) → Does she work
6. ___ she a doctor?
→ профессия (a doctor) → Is she a doctor
7. ___ they cooking dinner?
→ cooking (-ing) → Are they cooking
8. ___ he drive a car?
→ drive — действие → Does he drive
04

Drill · Present Simple or Continuous?

Simple — what's usual, regular, always. Markers: every day, usually, often, sometimes, never. Continuous — what's happening right now. Markers: now, at the moment, look!

1. Right now, she ___ in the park.
→ right now → Continuous → is walking
2. I usually ___ coffee in the morning.
→ usually → Simple → drink
3. Look! It ___ outside.
→ Look! = прямо сейчас → Continuous → is raining
4. He ___ to work every day.
→ every day → Simple → walks (3 лицо + s)
5. They ___ a movie at the moment.
→ at the moment → Continuous → are watching
6. She always ___ her keys at home.
→ always → Simple → forgets
7. "Where is John?" — "He ___ dinner now."
→ now → Continuous → is cooking
8. I never ___ milk in my coffee.
→ never → Simple → put
05

Article · the with places

Simple rule: the + specific place (the bank, the park, the museum). No article — work, home, school, bed (when it's the routine, not the building).

1
I go to park every Sunday.
2
She goes to work at 9 a.m. (если без артикля — поставь длинное тире —)
3
The shop is opposite bank.
4
He stays at home in the evening.
5
My children go to school by bus.
6
Wait for me at bus stop next to the café.
7
She is sitting in kitchen now.
8
We go to bed late on Fridays.

Запомни связку: work, home, school, bed — без the (когда говорим про процесс, не про здание). Все остальные конкретные места — the: the park, the bank, the museum, the post office, the corner, the bus stop, the café.

06

Role-play · you are local · give directions

Imagine a tourist comes up to you asking for directions. Use the words from section 1. Explain each route in 4–5 sentences.

Карта в голове — ты стоишь у выхода из метро. Перед тобой главная улица. На углу справа — кафе. За кафе — банк. Дальше прямо — парк. Через дорогу от парка — музей. Слева от музея — почта.

1.Турист: "Excuse me, where is the post office?"
2.Турист: "How do I get to the park?"
3.Турист: "Is the museum near here?"
4.Турист: "Where can I get a coffee?"
▾ Модельный ответ на #1 (post office)
"Go straight on this street. Turn right at the corner — there is a café. The bank is next to the café. Continue straight to the park. The post office is opposite the museum, on the left."
07

Mini-write · your route to the nearest café

Write 5–7 sentences: how to walk from your house to the nearest café. Use go straight, turn left/right, next to, opposite, on the corner. Simple sentences only — don't overcomplicate.

Цель: 5–7 предложений, минимум 3 разных предлога/направленияwords: 0
08

Speaking finale · 4 questions about your city

Answer each question aloud in a full sentence. No short "yes/no". Tap ▶ to hear the question first if you need.

1.What is near your house? Tell me three places. (Подсказка: There is/are a … near my house.)
2.Where do you usually go on Sunday morning? (I usually go to the …)
3.What are you doing right now? (I am … now.)
4.Is your city big or small? What do you like in it? (My city is … I like …)
