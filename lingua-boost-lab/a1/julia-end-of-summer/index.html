1

-ED pronunciation 🎧

The rule: three sounds for the -ed ending

1️⃣ /t/ — after voiceless sounds

after /p/ /k/ /f/ /s/ /ʃ/ /tʃ/ → watched, liked, washed, jumped

2️⃣ /d/ — after voiced sounds

after /b/ /g/ /v/ /z/ /m/ /n/ /l/ /r/ + vowels → played, lived, cleaned, opened

3️⃣ /ɪd/ — after /t/ or /d/

one extra syllable → wanted, started, visited, needed
